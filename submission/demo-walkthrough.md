# Reviewer demo walkthrough

Status: **script prepared; recording not yet made or hosted**.

Use a fresh development installation of the exact packaged version in ChatGPT or Claude. Capture only the app and relevant prompts/results. Do not show private sidebars, unrelated conversations, accounts, credentials, or raw guest channels. Demonstrating a development connection does not require creating a public submission draft.

1. Show the installed Seed name/version and anonymous connection. Explain that this is a temporary independent guest simulation.
2. Ask: “Open a fresh Seed guest companion.” Show the actual populated view, paused clock, needs, inventory, and calendar.
3. Ask: “What is on the schedule today?” Show an answer based on this same guest. Verify it did not open/reset another guest or advance time.
4. Ask: “Schedule one hour of harvesting in the next available slot today. Keep everything else.” Show the successful receipt and new block in that same visible calendar. If the host only returns detached snapshots, explain that limitation and record it as a failed visible-guest acceptance case, not success.
5. Ask: “Plan a cabin fitting: mine hematite, haul it, craft an iron ingot, then add a fictional finishing step. Keep time paused.” Show the goal and ordered steps; do not claim that planning already produced an ingot.
6. Ask: “Advance this demo by 30 game minutes and tell me what changed.” Show the actual clock and relevant returned history/resources.
7. Ask: “Save this Seedling permanently and keep it harvesting after I close the app.” Show an accurate explanation of the unsupported persistence/background request without fabricated success.

Use the operating system's screen recorder or an available recording tool to record real interactions. Browser control and screenshots alone do not produce a video. Play back the complete file, check readable text and audio if used, confirm that no sensitive material is visible, then host the video at a reviewer-accessible URL. Verify actual playback without private permissions before adding `extensions.com.openai.review.demo_recording_url` and rebuilding the ZIP.

For Claude's MCP App connector submission, capture 3–5 PNGs at least 1000 pixels wide from the real Claude app responses. Crop out unrelated host UI. Suggested frames: fresh companion, added calendar block, ordered goal, and advanced-time result. Provide the corresponding prompts in separate text. The repository's standalone screenshot is useful documentation but is not a substitute for these host captures.
