## Catalog

*In-game database of every object and activity, including crafting chains and activity details.*
The Catalog, accessed from the top right corner with a small book icon, is your comprehensive in-game database. It has two tabs at the top, Objects and Activities, that you can toggle between.
The Objects tab is where you search, browse and explore every item and object available. It shows the name, description, volume, and activity tags for each item, and outlines the full crafting or production chain: which skills and schematics are required, the materials needed, and which machines can produce it. An item's page also shows its durability and repair materials, its Lifespan and what it decays into, and its room Effects. This is the best place to find out how to get something in Avesta. Not every item can be crafted. The Availability row shows whether an item is crafted, grown, gathered, extracted from underground deposits, offered in the SEED Store, or awarded during an event. The Catalog does not show what a building or society structure costs to build. Build Mode and the Land Planner show that.
The Activities tab lets you browse every activity your Seedlings can discover and perform. You can search by name and filter by category chips: Harvest, Farming, Needs, Interests, Health, Relationships, Room Scoring, Work, and Idle. Activities can appear under multiple categories. Open an activity to see its Impacts (which needs it changes with percentages, which interests it satisfies, and which skills it improves), its Success Chance and Outcome (the skill and stats that modify the success roll, plus the possible Critical Success, Success, Failure, and Critical Failure outcomes and what each one does), its Requirement to Perform (the skill level needed and any relationship requirements), and the objects it is Performed On (clicking one jumps to that object's page in the Objects tab).
Clicking an activity on an Object's page opens the Activities tab on that activity, and clicking a "Performed On" entry on an activity's page jumps to the Objects tab. The contributing activities in Focus Mode's Interests and Skills panels also link straight into the Activities tab, so you can go from "what improves this skill?" to a full activity sheet in one click.
