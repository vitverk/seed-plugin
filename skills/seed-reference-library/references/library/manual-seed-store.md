## Seed Store

<!-- keywords: new seedling buy purchase another pollen life -->
*Real-money purchases: Pollen, Fertility Pills, Seedling Lives, and the Cultivator Inventory.*
The Seed Store lets you purchase items for use in-game with real world currency. It is different from the Marketplace in game, which allows you to buy items from Seed Industries or other Cultivators.

### Pollen
Pollen is a premium currency you can buy from SEED Store. Alternatively, you can trade SeedCoin for Pollen with other Cultivators on the Pollen Exchange. Pollen can be used to buy a Seedling Life for a new Seedling, a Fertility Pill for having kids, or Matsu and Bristlecone Tree Seeds (see Matsu and Bristlecone Trees). Every Cultivator has a single Pollen Wallet of their own; Pollen never moves directly between Cultivators — the only way to trade it is through the Pollen Exchange.

#### Pollen Exchange
The Pollen Exchange is where Cultivators trade Pollen and SeedCoin with each other. Open it by clicking your Pollen Wallet in the top-left corner of the HUD.

To get Pollen with SeedCoin, place a buy offer: you choose the SeedCoin price you are willing to pay for a fixed batch of Pollen. The batch size is set by the game and shown in the window — every offer is for the same amount, and only the price is up to you. The SeedCoin is reserved from a Seedling wallet you choose and is returned in full if you cancel. You can have only one buy offer active at a time; placing a new one replaces the old one and refunds its reservation. Offers never expire on their own: an offer stays up until a seller accepts it, you cancel or replace it, or the paying wallet can no longer cover the promised price — in that case the offer is automatically withdrawn.

To turn Pollen into SeedCoin, open the Exchange view and accept the current best offer from another Cultivator. The Pollen leaves your Pollen Wallet, and the offered SeedCoin is paid into a Seedling wallet you pick. An offer is always filled in one go — there are no partial trades — and you cannot accept your own offer. Buyers set their prices freely, so the exchange rate is whatever Cultivators agree on. The exchange spans all of Avesta: offers come from Cultivators in every society, not just your own. If your buy offer has not been accepted for a while, it usually means sellers are getting better prices elsewhere — try raising your offer, or ask around in the Players Chat.

#### Why Extra Seedlings Cost Pollen
SEED is a persistent world, meaning your Seedlings continue to work even when you are logged off. Pollen allows Seedlings to join this world, which runs 24/7 with constant societal evolution and simulation; you are not paying for competitive advantages over other Cultivators, as every society succeeds through cooperation and planning. The concept of permanent Seedlings is incompatible with Avesta because without natural lifecycles, resources would deplete and new players could not integrate; therefore, the generational system is necessary to keep societies dynamic and ensure all players have opportunities to lead, build, and shape the world.

### Cultivator Inventory
The Cultivator inventory is a unique Inventory (with no physical location) that will always be with you and is not dependent on your Seedlings or any society. You can find items you purchased from the SEED Store in your Cultivator Inventory. To access this, go to your Cultivator Profile in the top-left corner of the HUD. From your cultivator inventory, you can then send those items to Avesta to any storage container that your Seedling has access to. Seedling Lives, Seedling Slots and Fertility Pills can't be sent to Avesta. You use them from the + panel next to your Seedling Card or from the Cultivator Inventory.

### Getting More Seedlings: Slots, Lives and Babies
<!-- keywords: how many limit maximum add another second third -->
*How many Seedlings you can have, and the two ways to add one: a new Seedling (Seedling Life) or a baby (Fertility Pill).*
Every living Seedling takes a Seedling Slot. You can add a Seedling in two ways. Create a new one with a Seedling Life item, or have a baby with a Fertility Pill. Both need a free Seedling Slot.

#### Seedling Slots
<!-- keywords: slot item full expired ran out cannot create seedling limit reached bronze silver gold platinum -->
Your Seedling Slots set how many living Seedlings you can have at once, across all your societies. Babies take a slot too. Click the + in the bottom left of the screen, next to your Seedling Card, to see them.
Extra slots come as items, purchased from the SEED Store or rewarded at certain Cultivator Levels. Initially, a slot item does nothing while it sits in your Cultivator Inventory. Open the Cultivator Profile, go to Cultivator Inventory and activate it there. From then on, some slot items will last a set time and some will never expire. The countdown starts when you activate a slot, not when you receive it, so you can hold an unneeded slot item until you want it. The Seedling Slot Items topic lists every slot item and how long it lasts.
A slot stays in use while the Seedling in it is alive. It frees up when that Seedling dies. Nothing happens to your Seedlings when a timed slot runs out. They stay exactly as they are. You just cannot create another Seedling until you are back within the slots you have.

#### A New Seedling: Seedling Life
<!-- keywords: seedling life buy spawn create new character -->
A Seedling Life pays for one new Seedling. It does not give you an extra slot. You get Seedling Lives from the SEED Store or as rewards at certain Cultivator Levels. If your last Seedling dies, you get a Seedling Life for free.
To add a Seedling, click the + icon in the bottom-left corner of your screen and click an empty slot ("Click to add a Seedling"). Then, customize their appearance, stats and personality traits. This creates the Seedling only in your current society. You cannot choose another society with the + button.

#### A Baby: Fertility Pill
<!-- keywords: take use give pregnant pregnancy fertile pollen pill plant icon kids children -->
Becoming Fertile and getting Pregnant are two separate steps toward having a baby. Here is what having a baby requires:
- A romantic partner: the two Seedlings must be Partners. Dating is not enough. Only Partners can have children together.
- Fertility: use a Fertility Pill on the Seedling of the couple you want to carry the pregnancy. A Seedling of any gender can become pregnant. Using the pill makes that Seedling fertile immediately. You can check whether a Seedling is Fertile or Pregnant in Focus Mode's Body tab.
- Residency: your Seedling must be an assigned resident of a home. The first building your Seedling owns or rents is automatically set as their residence; if their residency was changed or removed, you can manage residents in the Properties Panel.
- A free Seedling Slot for the new baby, and room in your society's population. Both are checked when you use the Fertility Pill. From then on, the fertile or pregnant Seedling holds a slot for the baby.
Once all of the above are met, pregnancy itself is triggered by the couple's romantic activity on a double bed: select the partner Seedling and choose "Try for baby", or ask your Seedling in Seedling Chat. It can also happen while the two are talking with each other. A couple does not start it by themselves in their free time. Being Fertile only makes a Seedling eligible for that activity. It doesn't cause the pregnancy by itself.
The baby belongs to the Cultivator who owns the Seedling that carried the pregnancy. You can only use a Fertility Pill on your own Seedlings.

You get Fertility Pills from the SEED Store or as rewards at certain Cultivator Levels. They wait in your Cultivator Inventory until you use one. To use a pill, either:
- Click the + icon in the bottom-left corner of your screen and click the sprout icon next to the Seedling, then confirm "Make Fertile". This uses 1 Fertility Pill. A Seedling in another society shows a globe icon instead: visit that society to make them fertile.
- Open the Cultivator Profile, go to Cultivator Inventory, click "Make Fertile" on the pill and choose a Seedling. That panel also tells you why a Seedling cannot be chosen.
