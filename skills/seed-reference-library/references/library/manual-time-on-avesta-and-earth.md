## Time on Avesta and Earth

<!-- keywords: tau taus real time real hour real day game time in-game time sim time convert conversion avestan hour avestan year AT ET badge symbol sign planet icon hexagon arrow chevron circle cross earth time avesta time hover both clocks -->
*How Avesta time compares with Earth time, and what the Avesta and Earth signs in front of a time mean.*

Avesta time runs 24 times faster than Earth time, which is real-world time. A tau, a day on Avesta, lasts 1 Earth hour. An Avestan hour lasts 2.5 Earth minutes. An Avestan year has 24 taus and lasts 1 Earth day. To turn Avesta time into Earth time, divide by 24. To turn Earth time into Avesta time, multiply by 24: 30 Earth minutes are 12 Avestan hours. While Avesta time is paused, Earth time keeps running.

A planet sign in front of a time tells you which kind of time it is. The sign of the planet Avesta is a hexagon with an arrow pointing up inside. It marks Avesta time (AT). The sign of the planet Earth is a circle with a cross. It marks Earth time (ET). Earth time shows in your own time zone. Hover the time to see it on both clocks, with AT or ET next to each sign. The writer of a message picks the clock.
