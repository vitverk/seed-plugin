## Player/Cultivator

<!-- keywords: objective complete find -->
*Your role as Cultivator: profile, level progression, Players Chat, and objectives.*

You, the Cultivator, are the guiding force behind all your Seedlings. You are a planner and designer of life on Avesta, managing their needs, schedules, employment, society roles, and more. You are not seen by the Seedlings, but you can chat with them or give them commands through Seedling Chat. Remember, you play alongside other Cultivators, and communicating and engaging with others is key to helping your Seedlings survive the harsh conditions of Avesta. Everything you do and everything you create on the planet must be carried out by a Seedling in the end. From building things to moving them around, always keep in mind that all happenings on Avesta are completed by Seedlings.

### Cultivator Profile
Your profile includes your Cultivator Username, Cultivator Level and bio. This personalizes your presence in the world and helps other Cultivators know about you and can be viewed and edited through the top-left HUD.

#### Cultivator Level and Progression

Your Cultivator Level reflects your overall growth and experience. Unlike your Seedlings, your Cultivator Level stays with you across all societies and all Seedlings you guide - even after they died.

##### Earning XP
You can earn XP through these activities:
- **Completing Objectives:** Complete guided tasks in [Objectives].
- **Caring for Seedlings:** Maintain your Seedlings' well-being and stability over time.
- **Completing Seedling Objectives:** Fulfill your Seedling's life stage objectives to earn additional XP.

You don't need to achieve perfect stats or micromanage everything. The system rewards consistent care and long-term stability.

##### Rewards and Capacity
As you level up, you unlock rewards like cosmetic items, practical items, and economic benefits. Some levels also reward a Seedling Slot item or a Seedling Life, which let you add more Seedlings (see "Getting More Seedlings: Slots, Lives and Babies").

There is no single "best" way to level up. Whether you play casually or deeply, your progress is your own. Cultivator Levels unlock conveniences and options rather than giving you power over other players, keeping the Avesta life balanced for everyone.


### Players Chat
*Chatting with other Cultivators in your society and across the globe.*
<!-- keywords: direct message dm private message whisper friend channel invite -->

The Chat is located in the right-bottom of your screen. It allows you to communicate with other Cultivators both within your own society and across the globe.

You can send a private message to another Cultivator. First add them as a friend with Find Friends. Then click their name and choose Message. You can also click Chat on their Spark profile.

You can also create a channel, a chat room for several Cultivators. Click Create Channel and choose Public or Private. Anyone can find a Public channel with Find Channel. A Private channel can't be found. You can only invite your friends. A channel is not a Group. Groups are for Seedlings and are in the Groups app.

The chat is a crucial tool for collaboration and socialization. You can use it to ask questions about gameplay, find help with challenges, and build a strong community. Since each society has different conditions, the Players Chat is also a great resource for getting inspiration and learning how others are completing objectives, constructing buildings, and developing their Seedlings.

#### Chat Mini Mode
The Mini Chat Mode is available on your screen by default. You can enlarge it to window mode to see more conversations and details. You can close and reopen the chat at any time by clicking the chat button in the dock. You can pin important conversations in the main chat window so they remain easily accessible. For longer conversations, managing contacts, or viewing full chat history, open the main Chat Window to access the complete view. Chat size, notifications, and other display preferences can be adjusted by clicking the Settings button within the chat interface.

### Cultivator Objectives
*The Objectives panel: challenges that teach you how to play, and a place to claim objective rewards.*

Objectives are guided tasks that teach you how to play. They are not achievements - that's a separate feature with different panels and rewards. You can find the Objectives panel in the top-right corner of your screen by clicking the 'scroll' icon. Claim objective rewards in the [Objectives] panel.

If you get stuck on an objective, don't hesitate to ask PAM or other Cultivators for help! Remember that every society and environment is different, which can impact how and when you complete your objectives. A good strategy is to read the instructions carefully, and reach out to PAM or other Cultivators if you need assistance.
