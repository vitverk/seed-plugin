This section covers society structures.

## Society Structures

<!-- keywords: kernel spaceport start new society road subway orbital storage migrate migration -->
*The Society Kernel, migrating to another society, the Spaceport with the Global Market and Orbital Storage, roads, and subways.*
Structures are any kind of built object/building that is a part of your society.

### Society Kernel
The Society Kernel is a central building where new Seedlings arrive when they are sent to the planet or migrate from another society. It also serves as a communal storage container for the resources needed to start a new society. New societies begin with limited infrastructure, so establishing food production and jobs quickly is crucial for survival. The Society Kernel is also involved in migrating a Seedling to another society. To start, browse to the target society in World View and open its Society Overview panel, then click "Migrate Here". Migration requires a Migration Ticket. Your Seedling receives some Migration Tickets when they are created. Seedlings cannot migrate if they are a child, pregnant, or have dependent children (infants or kids). You will also be warned if your Seedling owns real estate, owns a business, holds a society role, leads a group, or has active relationships: be sure to prepare your Seedling for the change beforehand.

#### Starting your Own Society
To found a new society, you need a group of at least [world-dependent value: min lobby size] Cultivators.(May not apply: founding tickets enabled.)  Each member needs a Founding Ticket, which is spent only when the society is founded. See the Founding Tickets section for details. You can either create your own lobby or join someone else's lobby. If you create your own lobby, you can choose a location on the map for your new society. Pay close attention to the available resources on the map: water is crucial for early survival, and choosing a location with scarce water will make things significantly harder. You can set your lobby to private. Only Cultivators you share its invite link with can join a private lobby, and the host sets a password for the new society before founding it.

Once [world-dependent value: min lobby size] Cultivators have gathered in the lobby, you can proceed with creating a new Seedling (you need a Seedling Life and an available Seedling Slot). Early societies require close collaboration between all members, so communicate actively and play together to ensure your society's survival.

#### Migrating to Another Society
<!-- keywords: migrate migration move to another society switch leave join different society migration ticket belongings take along keep organizations window all societies rocket -->
*How a Seedling moves from its current society to another one.*
(May not apply: organization browser enabled.) In the Organizations window, select the Societies tile and switch to All Societies. Select the target society, then click the rocket button next to its name, Migrate here. In World View, select the target society and choose "Migrate Here". You can also click your own Society Kernel and choose "Migrate", which needs no approval from your current society's leadership. Migrating an existing Seedling uses a Migration Ticket. If the target society is public, you can select "Visit" first to look around in Visitor Mode before deciding. Items stored in your Seedling's properties stay in your current society. If your society has a Spaceport, move them to Orbital Storage before migrating. Use the "Go to Orbital Storage" button in the migration window. You can bring them down again in any society with a Spaceport.

### Spaceport
<!-- keywords: orbital storage unlock global market build spaceport get items back retrieve take out bring down stored off world items returned failed delivery -->
*The structure that unlocks the Global Market and Orbital Storage.*
The Spaceport can be built from within the Land Planner (switch to Spaceport from the bottom left) and is one of the major society structures. The Spaceport is not available at the start of a society, but must be built and serves as a major milestone of a society’s progress. It is essential for accessing the Global Market, which lets societies trade goods with each other, and for using Orbital Storage. It is expensive to build but critical for advanced societies that seek interaction with other regions of Avesta. Its material cost is fixed and shown in the Land Planner before you place it.
Once your society has a Spaceport, you can access Orbital Storage through the Properties Panel. Select the item you want to move and click the "Move to Orbital Storage" button in the bottom right. You can do this at any time. It is not only for migrating, though it is useful then, because anything you store follows you to your new society.
To bring something back down, pick the stored item and choose a container you can receive deliveries into in the society you are in. It is not instant. The delivery time you see is in Avesta time, which runs far faster than real time, so it arrives much sooner than the figure suggests.
If a delivery from Orbital Storage fails or only partly arrives, the items that were not delivered go back to your Orbital Storage. A notification, "Items returned to Orbital Storage", says how many. Items a Seedling or drone already picked up and then lost on the way are not returned.

### Roads
Roads can be built from within the Land Planner (switch to Roads from the bottom left) and are one of the society structures. They cost SeedCoins to build and can be freely placed down over empty spots within a society to connect buildings, subway stations and other important points of interest.
Roads give Seedlings clear walking paths and are particularly useful in dense or maze-like societies. Roads also provide aesthetic structure and are useful in long-term planning. Only roads placed on the society's own territory count towards the Paved Streets society goal.

### Subways
Subways can be built from within the Land Planner (switch to Subways from the bottom left) and are one of the society structures. Subway stations speed up intra-society travel. They cost resources to build and must be strategically placed. Each station's material cost is fixed and shown in the Land Planner before you place it. Each society may build up to 30 subway stations. The first station can go anywhere. Every station after it must be within 300 metres of the nearest existing station. You cannot start a new station while another is still under construction. Only finished subway stations count towards the Going Underground society goal. Subway access is a major upgrade for medium to large societies with growing infrastructure.
