## Mining and Ore Processing

<!-- keywords: mine mining ore deposit extract extractor underground raw copper iron metal ingot process smelt stone dust -->
*Mining deposits and processing what you mine, such as ore into metal or Stone into Stone Dust.*
Mining is how your society gets minerals. Deposits give ores and raw minerals like Clay, Coal and Stone. They never give finished metals. A Chalcopyrite Deposit gives Chalcopyrite Ore, and a machine then processes that ore into Raw Copper. Raw metals (Raw Copper, Raw Iron and the like) and ingots are never mined directly. They come from processing ore, or from recycling Scrap Metal at a Basic Anvil. A material's name does not say where it comes from. Check its crafting chain in the [ObjectCatalog] instead of guessing. As a Cultivator you plan the chain, while your Seedlings do the work.

### How to Mine
1. Find a deposit. Deposits you can see on the surface, such as Chalcopyrite, Hematite, Coal, Clay and Stone Deposits, are scattered across the landscape. Click one to see what it holds.
2. Mine it by hand. Use Direct Control, click the deposit and pick its gathering option, for example "Mine Coal". Any adult Seedling can do this, with no machine or tool needed. A deposit runs out after enough mining and does not come back.
3. Go underground for more. Hidden deposits must first be found by scanning with a Resource Scanner (see Resource Scanning). Only these need a Mineral Extractor. It can be placed anywhere outdoors where you may build. What it produces depends on the ground below. The richer the deposit, the more often it yields the resource. The rest of the time it yields Stone Dust. Mining wears the deposit down step by step. The resource then comes less often. Once only Stone Dust comes out, scan for a new deposit and place an extractor there. Extracting Hematite, Chalcopyrite, Bauxite, Rutile, Aurite or Argentite ore needs a Seedling with the Ore Mining skill, which Harvesting unlocks (see Skills Reference). Other extracted resources, such as Coal, Clay and Stone, need no Ore Mining, and neither does mining by hand.

### Processing Ores
1. Bring the ore to a Mineral Separator, which processes ores into raw metals, for example Chalcopyrite Ore into Raw Copper. Some ores also need an acid.
2. Create a production task on the machine and send a Seedling to work on it, the same way as crafting (see Crafting and Production Tasks).
3. Smelt raw metals into ingots at a metalworking machine. Ingots are then forged into plates, rods and nails.
4. Other mined minerals are processed the same way. For example, Stone is ground into Stone Dust at a Masonry Bench.

For each metal ore's exact machines, amounts and skills, see Ore Processing. The [ObjectCatalog] also shows each material's full crafting chain.
