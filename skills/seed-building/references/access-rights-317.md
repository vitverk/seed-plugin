# Access Rights

**Ownership vs Access**

In Avesta, **owning** something means your Seedling can edit or transfer ownership of it, like a building or plot you placed; others can't change it. For **movable items** (furniture, food, tools) it's less about ownership and more about **access**: if your Seedling has access to a shower or a storage container, they can freely take things from it, even if they don't "own" them. This matters early on. A hungry Seedling that can access food will take it! If you don't want your things consumed by others, store them in an inventory with no public access.

**Roles and Permissions**

Permissions control what actions a Seedling or organization can perform, and they are **always granted through roles**. Permission categories include: Location (entry, security, residents, services, construction, listings), Object (storage, machines, delivery routing, moving, destroying), Society (voting, owning/renting property, territory, departments), Organization (creating/assigning roles, job postings), Wallet (spending organization funds), Plot (boundaries, roads, installations), Business (contracts, ownership transfer), and Group (invitations, disbanding). Some permissions additionally **require ownership**: e.g., you can only sell or transfer a building if the organization you act through actually owns it. Check what a role grants in the Societies Panel or the organization's management UI.

**Room Security**

In Business Branches used as service locations, you can set rooms as **private** to keep guests out: expand the rooms panel from the Inspector panel by clicking a room. You cannot set a room private if it's the only room of an opened service business, or if it's the only entrance to the building.

Related: a greyed out "Go here" usually means your Seedling lacks access to that location; public service locations are available to all Seedlings; and anyone with access to a building also has access to its inventories.
