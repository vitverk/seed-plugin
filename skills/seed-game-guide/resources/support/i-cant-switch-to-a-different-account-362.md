# I can't switch to a different account

1. Log out from Settings in the game or launcher, closing the window alone won't do it.
2. Make sure it's fully closed in the background, not just off screen.
3. Before logging back in, clear your browser's cookies for the login page, or use a private/incognito window.
4. Log in with the account you want.
