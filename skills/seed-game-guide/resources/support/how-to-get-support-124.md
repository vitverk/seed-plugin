# How to Get Support

## **Need help with SEED? You have options!**

Whether you need a quick answer about gameplay or help with a complex technical issue, there are three ways to get support.

## 1. Ask PAM (AI Assistant)

For immediate answers to gameplay questions, start here. PAM (Personal Assistant Module) is our in-game personal AI assistant, updated with the latest game knowledge.

- **How to access:** Click the **purple PAM icon** located at the end of the toolbar on the top-left side of your screen. ![](https://public-files.eu.theymes.com/klang-games/316ec6f3-55d1-44c5-9b48-2a5b98de563e.png)
- **How to use:** Simply type your question as you would with any AI chatbot. She will tailor her response specifically to your query.

## 2. Contact a Support Agent

If you cannot resolve your issue with PAM, you can reach our human support team in two ways:

- **Via the Support Page:** Click the **"**[**Contact Us**](https://klang-games.eu.theymes.com/hc/en/seed/contact-us)**"** button at the top of this page and fill out the request form.
- **Inside the Game:** You can submit a ticket directly from within SEED. ***Tip**: Submitting in-game is recommended for bugs, as it automatically includes helpful metadata from your game session.*

### What happens next?

1. Your request creates a ticket in our system.
2. A human team member reviews your request.
3. We aim to respond within **24 hours or less**. ***Please note:** Weekend requests may take longer due to reduced capacity.*

## 3. Community Support

Join the conversation with other Cultivators! While our community is mainly focused on discussing game strategies and gameplay, they are often happy to help with general questions.

- **Where to go:** [Join our Discord!](about:blank)
- **Channel:** Reach out in **#general-chat**. ***Tip:** If your problem appears to be complex or involves account issues, please skip Discord and reach out directly to the support team!*
