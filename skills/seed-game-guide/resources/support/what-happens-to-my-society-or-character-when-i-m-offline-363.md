# What happens to my society or character when I'm offline?

SEED is one living world that never stops. When you log off, Avesta keeps running,  time passes and life goes on. Your Seedling carries on with their day: working, resting, spending time with others, and making their own choices while you're away. That's the heart of SEED, your Seedling's story continues whether you're watching or not.

And you won't miss the big moments: when something worth sharing happens, your Seedling might text you about it, and you'll get a notification so you can check back in and see how their world has moved on.
