# How to Join Our Discord Community

Get more out of the game and join us on Discord! Meet other players and get to know our wonderful community managers.

## Joining Discord

**Discord Link**: <https://discord.gg/seedgame>

Simply click the link above or copy it into your web browser to join our community.

## What You'll Find

### A Welcoming and Helpful Community

- **Friendly players** who are happy to help new Cultivators
- **Experienced players** sharing tips and strategies
- **Community managers** providing additional support and updates

### Community Guidelines

- Please make sure to familiarise yourself with our [community guidelines](https://klang-games.eu.theymes.com/hc/en/seed/articles/seed-discord-rules-and-guidelines-111) before joining
- Please use appropriate channels for different topics
