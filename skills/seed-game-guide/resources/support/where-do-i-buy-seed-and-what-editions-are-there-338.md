# Where do I buy SEED, and what editions are there?

SEED is bought directly from [seed.game](http://seed.game) and played through the standalone SEED Launcher on PC and Mac, with a mobile Companion App also available. It's a one-time purchase, no subscription required to keep playing.

On the web store you'll find three packs:

- **Starter Pack - €29.99.** Cultivator License (the SEED game client plus access to Avesta), 1 permanent Platinum Seedling Slot, 1 Seedling Life, and Cultivator track rewards that unlock as you level up.
- **Pioneers Pack - €59.99.** Everything in the Starter Pack, plus an extra Platinum Slot, 35 Pollen, another Seedling Life, and limited-time unique clothing available only during launch.
- **Buy 3, Get 1 Free - €89.96.** Four Starter Packs (a €119.96 value).

Bought inside the game rather than on the web store:

- **Membership -  €14.99/month** (in-game store): a one-off +2 Seedling Lives, and +5 Seedling Slots that renew each month while you're subscribed.

Pollen and everything bought with it lives in the in-game Pollen Store: Pollen packs from €5.99 (50) to €44.99 (500), plus Seedling Lives (49 Pollen), Fertility Pills (25 Pollen), and additional Seedling Slots (Bronze/Silver/Gold/Platinum).

For a full breakdown, see our monetization blog post: <https://seed.game/news-from-avesta/everything-you-need-to-know-about-seed-monetization>
