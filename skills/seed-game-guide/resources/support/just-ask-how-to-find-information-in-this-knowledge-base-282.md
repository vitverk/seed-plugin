# Just Ask! – How to find information in this knowledge base

**Stop scrolling and start asking!** To give you the fastest help possible during our Open Beta, we’ve moved away from traditional lists of articles. Instead, our search bar uses an AI Assistant to find exactly what you need.

![](https://public-files.eu.theymes.com/klang-games/4e109c90-8cd6-4e76-ad61-2bc67453bad1.png)

- **Tailored Answers:** When you type a question, the AI generates a specific answer tailored to your needs, rather than a generic guide.
- **A Source of Truth:** Most gameplay answers come directly from our official SEED Game Manual to ensure you get the most accurate info on Avesta.
- **Support-Led Guides:** For non-gameplay topics (like account or technical issues), our support agents have created specialized articles for the AI to reference.
- **Be Specific:** You can ask complex questions like *"Why is my Seedling not harvesting marked trees?"* or *"How do I set up a Business Branch?"* and get an immediate explanation.

**Need more help?** If you can’t find what you’re looking for, please feel free to get in touch! You can reach us through the **“Contact us”** button at the top of this page.
