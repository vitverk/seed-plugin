# How do I contact support / report something?

You can reach us through the SEED Community Portal ticket system at <https://klang-games.eu.theymes.com/hc/en/seed/contact-us>, where reports are reviewed by our human support agents. You can also reach us by email at [support@klang-games.com](mailto:support@klang-games.com), or through our official Discord.
