# My screen is flickering, or the graphics look wrong

1. Update your graphics driver.
2. Turn off V-Sync, or try a different display mode.
3. Close anything that overlays your screen: Discord, GeForce Experience or Adrenalin, RGB lighting apps, recording software.
4. Reset your local SEED settings, it clears up most rendering glitches.
5. If it's only on one monitor, try a different cable or port.
