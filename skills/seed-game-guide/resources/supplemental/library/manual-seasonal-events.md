## Seasonal Events

*Limited-time events on real-world time, currently Dezima Donations and its leaderboard.*

Seasonal events are limited-time activities that run for all societies at the same time. Unlike most things in SEED, events run on real-world time, not in-game time. The countdown you see is real hours and days.

### Dezima Donations
The space station Dezima, the Cultivators' home above Avesta, has suffered a series of damaging incidents, and Seed Industries is collecting resources from all societies to rebuild it. Dezima Donations is a friendly competition between societies. Every donated part counts toward your society's total, and societies compete for the top spots on the leaderboard (see The Donation Leaderboard section for how the ranking works). It is a limited-time event. (May not apply: organization browser enabled.) The Dezima Donations section in the Organizations window (Alternative; may not apply.) The Dezima Donations tab in the Societies Panel shows while it's running. If you don't find it, the project might be paused.

#### How to Donate
1. Place a Dezima Donation Box anywhere in your society, indoors or outdoors. Donation Boxes can no longer be crafted, so only boxes made during the event exist.
2. Fill the box with the requested parts. The event currently asks for two kinds of parts. The advanced space-grade goods are Aramid Fabric, Fortified Glass, Kerosene, Teak Parquet, Ceramic Shield Tile, Board Computer, and Actuator; these take developed industry and skilled Seedlings to produce, so they are also a good trading opportunity for societies that can make them. The common industrial parts are Glass, Iron Bolt, Steel Beam, Steel Frame, Bearing, Synthetic Rubber, and Synthetic Fabric; these are simpler to make, so even societies with a young industry can contribute. The full list is always shown in the event description in the Society Window.
3. Select the box and press Donate. Everything in the box that matches the list is shipped to Dezima in one go and added to your society's donation totals. Shipped items are gone for good and leave Avesta.

Donations are credited to your society as a whole. It does not matter which Seedling crafts the parts or hauls them, or who presses Donate. If a donation fails to ship (for example, due to a connection problem), nothing is lost. The items stay in the box and you can simply press Donate again. Items that are reserved by a Seedling's activity are skipped. Once the event has ended, donations are no longer accepted. Your Donation Box stays and works as small storage.

#### The Donation Leaderboard
Open (May not apply: organization browser enabled.) your society's Dezima Donations section in the Organizations window (Alternative; may not apply.) the Dezima Donations tab in the Societies Panel to see how societies rank while the event runs. The ranking works like this.

- Each requested part is its own category. The society that donated the most of a part is that category's leader. If two societies are tied on amount, the one that started donating that part earlier leads.
- Societies are ranked first by how many categories they lead, and only then by the total number of items donated. A society that leads even one category ranks above any society that leads none, no matter how much the latter donated in total. Donating a lot of everything is good, but being the top donor of specific parts is better.
- The podium shows the top three societies and which parts they lead. The standings below list every society that has donated anything, along with your own society's rank.

Rewards have not been announced yet, so keep an eye on Spark for updates from Seed Industries.
