## First Steps

<!-- keywords: what should i do start beginner early game -->
*Recommended first activities after onboarding and how to earn your first SeedCoin.*

Avesta is a persistent world: your Seedlings continue to live, work, and follow their schedule even when you are logged off. Make sure their needs are taken care of and their schedule is set up before you leave!

Here are some great ways to get started in your Seedling's journey:
- Play Through the Objectives: Start by completing the Objectives that PAM gave you. This will teach you how to play the game and unlock new and exciting apps on your mobile device.
- Get to Know Your Seedling: After completing the Objectives, take some time to chat with your Seedling through Seedling Chat. You might discover what they're passionate about and what their desires are, which can guide your next steps.
- Find a Home: Consider visiting the Real Estate Market to see if there are available plots or buildings for sale. Having a home for your Seedling is a great foundation for their life on Avesta.
- Engage with Your Society: If you're interested in contributing to your society, reach out to the Society Leaders and other Cultivators through Players Chat. They can provide guidance and collaboration opportunities.

Societies Panel from the right sidebar to view the latest Society Goals and announcements from your Society Leader. This will help you understand what your society is working towards and how you might be able to contribute.

### How to Earn Your First SeedCoin
The easiest way to start earning SeedCoin is to find a job at an existing business: check the [Jobs] app to see if any Cultivators in your society are hiring. If no jobs are available, you can start your own business through the [BusinessApp], though this requires an initial investment. If you don't have the funds yet, get involved in your society! Check the [SocietyGoals] to see what your society is working towards and contribute. Participating in society development can open up opportunities to earn SeedCoin. You can also reach out to other Cultivators in [PlayersChat] to find collaboration opportunities.

If your society is in the early phase, focus on gathering Resources to meet your Seedling's basic needs for food and water. Raw algae from shrubs and raw lumber from trees are excellent starting points. Explore the Catalog to discover what items you can craft with these resources. You should also level up your Seedling's Skills so they can unlock new Schematics to craft useful items that satisfy their Needs and contribute to your society's development. As your society advances, don't hesitate to reach out to other Cultivators in Players Chat for assistance and collaboration.
