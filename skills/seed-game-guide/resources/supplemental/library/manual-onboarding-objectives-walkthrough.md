## Onboarding Objectives Walkthrough

*Step-by-step help for every onboarding objective, feature unlocks, and getting unstuck.*

When you start your journey, PAM guides you through a sequence of onboarding objectives, organized into small groups of tasks. Completing objectives earns Cultivator XP and gradually unlocks the apps and panels on your screen. Most of the interface starts hidden and appears as you progress. If you cannot see an app or panel that this manual mentions (for example the Scheduler, Jobs, Wallet, Focus Mode, Marketplace, or the (May not apply: organization browser enabled.) Organizations window (Alternative; may not apply.) Society Panel), the most likely reason is that you have not yet completed the objective that unlocks it. Keep following your objectives and it will appear.

The current objective is shown in the Objectives panel (top-right 'scroll' icon) and highlighted in the bottom bar. Expand an objective to read its full instructions; they contain the exact steps to follow.

### Getting Unstuck: The "I'm stuck" Button
<!-- keywords: skip skipping objective objectives XP experience rewards -->
If you cannot complete an onboarding objective, use the **"I'm stuck" button**: expand the current objective in the Objectives panel and the button is at the bottom of the expanded task info (it only appears on onboarding objectives you have not completed yet). It first asks what is keeping you stuck (can't find what to do, can't place the tent, can't find resources, another player is blocking you, and so on), gives a matching tip, and then offers one or more of these remedies depending on the reason:
1. **Skip this objective:** skips only the current task and moves you straight to the next one. The skipped objective still grants its XP. Cannot be undone.
2. **Skip all objectives:** skips the whole onboarding at once and unlocks all app features immediately. Skipped objectives still grant their XP. Cannot be undone. For Cultivators who want to explore freely without the guided path.
3. **Unlock all app features:** unlocks everything the onboarding would unlock, but keeps your objectives so you can still play through them at your own pace (and still earn their XP). Cannot be undone.
4. **Move to another society:** a shortcut to the migration interface, where you choose a new society for your Seedling. The right choice when another player is deliberately blocking your progress, for example when all nearby space is claimed.

You can also migrate without the button: click your own Society Kernel and choose "Migrate", which needs no approval from your current society's leadership.

General unstuck tips that solve most problems:
- An item the objective needs (Book, Herb Seeds, Survival Workbench, materials) is usually either in your Tent's Contents or in your Seedling's Inventory; check both.
- If a placement is blocked, the ground must be flat, open, and not claimed by another Cultivator; step a few tiles over and try again.
- If a resource can't be found nearby, walk your Seedling around; moving through nature reveals areas you cannot see yet.
- If an action button is grayed out, the object may be in use, owned by someone else, or missing required materials nearby.

### The Objective Sequence and Feature Unlocks
The onboarding objectives form one sequence: Look Around, Meet Your Seedling, Settle In, Build & Craft, Farming, Planning, Skills & Focus, and Relationships. Key features unlock at these points:
- Seedling Chat: after locating your Seedling.
- Seedling Inventory: after checking your Seedling's needs.
- Real Estate Market: after claiming your first plot.
- Catalog: after placing the Survival Workbench.
- Scheduler: after watering your crops.
- Focus Mode, Management Hub, view modes and the clock: after planning rest in the Scheduler.
- Seed Store and Pollen: after exploring your skills.
- Jobs app and Wallet: after improving a skill.
- Groups, Map View, World View, Marketplace, the Social app, Achievements, Society Goals and (May not apply: organization browser enabled.) the Organizations window (Alternative; may not apply.) the Societies Panel: after scheduling skill practice.
- Ballots(May not apply: !organization browser enabled.)  and the Business Panel: after the Talk to Someone objective.

### Onboarding: Look Around (camera basics)
- **Move the Camera:** hold and drag the right mouse button, or use the W, A, S, D keys.
- **Rotate the Camera:** use Q and E, or hold and drag the middle mouse button.
- **Zoom the Camera:** scroll the mouse wheel.
These objectives have a "Practice" button that launches a short camera practice mode.

### Onboarding: Meet Your Seedling
- **Locate Your Seedling:** click the locate button at the bottom left. It sits below your Seedling's portrait and looks like a map pin. Pressing the spacebar does the same.
- **Say Hello:** open the Seedling Chat at the left of your screen (the speech-bubble icon) and send your Seedling a message. You can ask about their past, their mood, or just make small talk.
- **First Steps:** click on the ground and select "Go here" to move your Seedling (this is called direct control). Double-clicking anywhere also works. Your Seedling may not have access to every place you can see; buildings can belong to other players.
- **Check Your Needs:** at the bottom left, to the bottom-right of the portrait, there is a group of colorful dots; click them to view your Seedling's Needs.

### Onboarding: Settle In (tent, food, and water)
- **Open Your Inventory:** click the backpack icon below the portrait at the bottom left.
- **Claim a Plot:** open the Seedling Inventory at the bottom left, select the Tent, and place it on the ground. The Tent only fits where there is free space; if plants or other Cultivators' locations block you, keep looking for a free spot.
- **Gather Food:** use direct control to harvest food from the wild. Click on mushrooms or shrubs and select the harvest action. There are different shrub types: Herbs, Algae and Berries. Harvested food can be eaten directly or cooked into something better. This objective needs three harvests to complete, so if it does not complete after the first one, just keep harvesting.
- **Chat Command:** open the Seedling Chat and give your Seedling a command, for example "Harvest mushrooms" or "Harvest something to eat from nature". What works depends on what is nearby; if a command fails, try something else based on the area.
- **Gather Water:** use direct control on a water source (look for ponds) and choose the fetch action. If there are no ponds near your tent, explore the surrounding area with your Seedling. This objective needs two fetches to complete.
- **Store Your Resources:** click on the Tent and choose "Drop Off" to store items from your Seedling's inventory. Food stored in your Tent can be eaten by your Seedling when hungry, and other Seedlings cannot access it.

### Onboarding: Build & Craft (workbench and furniture)
- **Place a Survival Workbench:** click on your Tent, select Contents, click the Survival Workbench inside to place it down. If it is not in the Tent, check your Seedling's inventory. If you already placed it, you can skip this task.
- **Assemble the Workbench:** placed objects need to be built before use. Use direct control: click the placed Workbench and choose "Assemble Object".
- **Craft a Crude Wood Stool:** click the Survival Workbench, create a new task, and select the Crude Wood Stool. The crafting materials need to be in your Tent. Then click the Workbench again and tell your Seedling to produce the item.
- **Place the Stool:** click the Workbench, tell your Seedling to pick up the Stool, open the Seedling's Inventory, and click the Stool's icon to place it. If the Stool is not in the Workbench, your Seedling may have carried it to the Tent or still have it in their inventory.
- **Assemble the Stool:** click the placed Stool with direct control and choose "Assemble Object", just like the Workbench.

### Onboarding: Farming
- **Place a Farm Field:** click on the ground next to your Tent, click the button for creating a Farm Field, click the ground to choose its location, and confirm.
- **Plow a Farm Field:** click the Farm Field you created (it looks like a blue rectangle on the ground) and tell your Seedling to plow it.
- **Plant a Seed:** click the field again, select the Herb Seeds, then tell your Seedling to plant them. The Herb Seeds need to be inside your Tent.
- **Water Your Crops:** click the field and tell your Seedling to water it. This requires Murky Water in the Tent; if you have none, collect water from nature and drop it off at the Tent first. Completing this unlocks the Scheduler.

### Onboarding: Planning (the Scheduler)
- **Plan for Farming:** open the Scheduler at the left of your screen, click on free space to create a new Schedule Block, click "add" to add a Priority, select Farming, then choose "Anything" or "Herbs". Your Seedling will follow the schedule and farm at the chosen time. Remember: every real hour is one full tau for your Seedling.
- **Craft a Storage Platform:** same crafting flow. Click the Survival Workbench, create a task, select the Storage Platform (materials must be in the Tent), then tell your Seedling to produce it.
- **Place the Storage Platform:** have your Seedling pick it up from the Workbench, open their Inventory, and click its icon to place it. Then click the Storage Platform, open its Settings with the gear icon, and tick Herbs (under Foods, then Raw). A new Storage Platform accepts nothing until you do this. Your Seedling fills it when they have a Haul priority, or when you tell them to haul with Direct Control.
- **Plan for Rest:** create another schedule block, choose "Address Need" as the priority and select Rest. Your Seedling will sleep at that time every tau.

### Onboarding: Skills and Focus
- **Open Focus Mode:** open Focus Mode from the bottom right of your screen.
- **Explore Your Skills:** open the Skills tab inside Focus Mode. Skills improve through practice; the more your Seedling does an activity, the better they get.
- **Read a Book:** click your Tent, open its Contents, click the Book inside and ask your Seedling to read it. Reading books increases skills. If the Book is not in the Tent, check the Seedling's inventory.
- **Improve a Skill:** keep reading until a skill reaches a new level, or gather more food to level the gathering skill instead. Completing this unlocks the Jobs app and the Wallet.
- **Schedule Skill Practice:** create a schedule block with the "Practice Skill" priority.

### Onboarding: Relationships
- **Open the Social App:** open the Social app from the left side of your screen. It tracks the relationships your Seedling grows with others.
- **Talk to Someone:** click on another Seedling, then choose an activity; your Seedling will approach them. If there are no other Seedlings around, you can skip this task. Completing it unlocks Ballots(May not apply: !organization browser enabled.)  and the Business Panel.
- **Reach Out to PAM:** open the PAM window. PAM is always there for you; ask her anything, anytime.

### Onboarding: Explore Your Society
- **Open Society Window:** (May not apply: organization browser enabled.) click Organizations on the right side of the screen and open your society (Alternative; may not apply.) click Societies on the right side of the screen to see how your Society is doing. Everything your Society achieves, you achieve together.
- **Check Your Society Goals:** open (May not apply: organization browser enabled.) your society's Goals section in the Organizations window (Alternative; may not apply.) the Society Goals tab in the Societies Panel to see what to work towards next. Completing goals advances your Society to the next tier.
