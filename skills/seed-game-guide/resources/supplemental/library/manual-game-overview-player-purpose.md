## Game Overview & Player Purpose

This is the game manual. The “you” in this section refers to the Cultivator.

<!-- keywords: goal purpose playing as role -->
*What SEED is about and your goal as a Cultivator guiding Seedlings.*

SEED is a society simulation where you, the Cultivator, guide the lives of your Seedlings in a massive, persistent world called Avesta. Your actions have a significant impact on your society. The choices your Seedlings make, from their careers to their relationships, shape the world for all other players. Your ultimate goal is to guide your Seedlings as they are born, grow, and eventually die, helping them achieve their dreams. Their legacy is your creation. A day on Avesta is called a tau. One tau equals one real-world hour, and a year on Avesta is 24 taus.
