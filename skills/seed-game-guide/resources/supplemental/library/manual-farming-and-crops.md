## Farming and Crops

<!-- keywords: produce food grow crop early mushroom seeds farm mushrooms -->
*Placing farm fields, planting, watering, and harvesting crops for food.*
Farming is the best way to secure food for your society. As a Cultivator, you act as the planner, while your Seedlings do the physical work on Avesta. Farm fields can be placed anywhere on your owned plots and on a building's Outdoor Block tiles (its garden or yard), but not inside a building. You can grow food crops like Wheat, Tomatoes, Corn, and Pumpkins, as well as Sapling Seeds, a much slower-growing Farm Field crop that produces Raw Lumber rather than food. Don't confuse this farmed crop with the tree saplings you plant directly in the world (see the World Resources section) - same word, two different things: the Farm Field crop is harvested on your plot for Raw Lumber, while a directly-planted sapling stays in the world and slowly grows into a harvestable tree. There are no mushroom seeds. Mushrooms cannot grow in a farm field. Harvest them in the wild, or grow them from Biomass in a Hydroponic Growbox.

### How to Start a Farm
To begin, click on a plot you own and select Create Farm Field to enter farming mode. Once you place the field, follow these steps:
1. Plow: Use direct control to have your Seedling plow the field to get it ready.
2. Select seeds: Click on the farm field and "Plant Seeds". Choose which crop you want to grow. You must have the matching Seed Bag in your inventory or a nearby storage container.
3. Use Direct Control and send your Seedling to plant the seeds. Your Seedling will then pick up the bag and plant the seeds.
4. Water: To keep crops growing, you must provide Murky Water. You can find water deposits in nature. Keep a steady supply in nearby storage so your Seedlings can water the fields.

### Grow Your Crops and Harvesting
<!-- keywords: how much water how often schedule watering hours, harvest crops priority -->
Different crops have different water needs and grow at different speeds — see Crop Yields for exact per-crop numbers, including how many hours a full watering lasts before growth stalls. If you run out of Murky Water, your crops will stop growing. Crops grow slowly over time. You can watch them progress from tiny sprouts to full-grown plants. Once your crops are fully grown, a Seedling needs to harvest them. Use the Farm priority in the Scheduler. It covers plowing, planting, watering and harvesting. The Harvest priority is for wild resources, not crops. These vegetables can be used to cook high-quality meals and snacks. Check the Catalog to see which schematics you can use with your harvest.

Pro-Tip: Before you log out, always check that your storage has enough Murky Water and that your Seedlings have the right instructions so your farm stays healthy while you are away!
