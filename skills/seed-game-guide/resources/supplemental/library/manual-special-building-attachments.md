## Special Building Attachments

<!-- keywords: campus residence billboard society-owned department-owned -->
*Building attachments such as the Campus Residence Billboard.*
Attachments are just objects that can be added onto buildings on the outside, like on the roof or outer walls. There are also special buildings that have unique functionality like:
- Campus Residence Billboard: You can equip a building in your society with a Campus Residence Billboard, converting it into a public residence for all Seedlings that do not yet have a home. It is available in the Marketplace at a hefty price. Having a campus can ensure that new Seedlings have a place to stay when they first arrive. This is especially useful if a large amount of your society's land is privately owned. A campus helps new arrivals feel less stressed as they settle in.
