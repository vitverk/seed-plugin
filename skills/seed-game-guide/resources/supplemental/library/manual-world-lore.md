## World & Lore

<!-- keywords: avesta dezima planet place world where am i -->
*The planet Avesta, Seedling origins, societies, and background lore.*

Avesta is a vibrant, Earth-like planet orbiting Tau Ceti, inhabited by Seedlings: human clones unaware they were created on an interstellar ark called Dezima. The human player, known as the Cultivator, guides the Seedlings from Dezima: mentors the Seedlings recognize and rely on to shape their lives and societies. Most Seedlings originate in Verdantis, especially the Eilo Archipelago, a tropical chain of over 500 islands rich in bioluminescent flora, interconnected fungal networks, and dangerous plant life. Seedlings can physically walk to a nearby society, but they cannot interact with that society's Seedlings or objects. To participate in another society, a Seedling must migrate there. Seedling societies, or unihavens, are experimental in nature, ranging from the authoritarian, agrarian Prosperia Prime to the democratic and creative Novalith. The sprawling metropolis of Metahaven stands as a pinnacle of Seedling achievement, divided between engineering and artistic elites and shadowed by the hedonistic underworld of the Core Pass. Cultural life flourishes with immersive holobooks, sensory concerts, philosophical schools inspired by Leibniz, and entertainment from reality shows like “Barry In…” to strategy games like Ariel’s Gambit.
