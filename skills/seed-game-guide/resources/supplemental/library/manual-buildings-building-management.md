This section covers buildings & building management.

## Buildings & Building Management

*Player-built buildings and every building management tool, from construction to business operations.*

This section will cover the types of player created buildings as well as all the management options available within buildings. To see the management options for a building you own, select it and open its context menu, then choose Manage.

### Unknown Buildings and Discovery
<!-- keywords: unknown building hasn't been here not visited yet discover explore -->
*What you see of a building your Seedling hasn't been to, and how to send them there.*

Each Seedling only knows the buildings they have visited or heard about from another Seedling. Every Seedling knows different buildings, so switching Seedlings can change what you see.

Click a building your Seedling doesn't know and its menu says they haven't been here. You still see its name, opening hours and entry fee. Its rating and prices stay hidden until your Seedling visits.

To find out what a place offers, press "Visit to discover" and your Seedling goes there. If the button is greyed out, the reason shows under it, for example the place is closed. You can also pick "Schedule a visit" to plan the trip in the [Scheduler], or "View on Map" to look at the place in the [MapView] first.

A Seedling whose society role holds a permission over the society's buildings and plots, such as "Seedlings enter and use locations" or "See property pages", knows every building in the society, whatever it has discovered itself; if keeping an overview of the whole society matters to you, ask your leaders for such a role.

### Management Hub
<!-- keywords: panels apps access -->
*The right-side hub listing the main management panels and apps.*

The Management Hub is the group of buttons on the right side of the screen. (May not apply: organization browser enabled.) It holds Properties (the Properties Panel), Organizations (the Organizations window, where every society, business, group and department is managed) and Marketplace. (Alternative; may not apply.) It holds Properties (the Properties Panel), Business (the Business Panel), Societies (the Societies Panel) and Marketplace. Below it are Chat, Real Estate Market, Land Planner, SEED Store and, once one of your Seedlings has died, Memoria.

### Properties Panel
<!-- keywords: plot location find locate rename building access manage -->
*Managing your plots and buildings: details, residents, renaming, and locating them.*

The Properties Panel is your central space for managing the plots and buildings you have permission to. This also includes society-owned buildings if your role in the government gives you the right permission.
The buttons at the top switch what its list shows: Units (your territories, plots and buildings), Storage, Machines and Furniture(May not apply: !organization browser enabled.) , plus Deliveries and Market. In the Units list, each plot and building has a map pin, Locate, that moves the camera to it.(May not apply: organization browser enabled.)  Under each building, the list also has its Deliveries and Market pages, and Services and Contracts where you may manage them. Under each plot, it has the plot's Deliveries.
Select a plot or building to open its page. There you can rename it, and see its owner or administrator and use Change Owner. You can also manage the residents of a home, list it for sale or rent, and open it in Build Mode with Edit in Builder.(May not apply: !organization browser enabled.)  Cards at the bottom of a building's page open its deliveries and its Market page.

(May not apply: organization browser enabled.)

### The Organizations Window
<!-- keywords: organizations window browser where manage business group society department find migrate offer my organizations business panel societies panel society window business window moved gone -->
*The one window for browsing and managing every organization: societies, businesses, groups, and departments.*
Click Organizations in the Management Hub on the right side of the screen to open the Organizations window. Every society, business, group and department is browsed and managed here.

#### Organizations Window Tiles and My Organizations
<!-- keywords: rail tiles left side icons home page needs attention your seedlings badge number reorder drag order remember portraits faces -->
*The column of tiles on the left of the Organizations window, and the My Organizations page.*
A column of tiles runs down the left side of the window:
- My Organizations, the house icon at the top.
- One tile for each type of organization you belong to: Societies, Businesses, Groups and Departments. A type gets its tile once one of your Seedlings is in an organization of that type, or, for Departments, once your society role lets you manage one.
- New Organization, the plus tile at the bottom, for founding a new organization.

A number on a type's tile counts the ballots there that you can still vote in and have not opened yet. Drag the tiles to change their order. You can also drag the organizations in the list beside the tiles. The order is saved on this computer. While the game runs, the window reopens on the page you last had open under each tile.

Small portraits next to an organization's name show your Seedlings in it. The label after them counts them, such as "2 Employees". Point at a portrait to see the Seedling's name.

The My Organizations page has two cards. "Needs attention" lists what waits on you in your organizations. Examples are invitations to answer, job applications to review and ballots to vote in. It also lists offers and applications that end soon, empty positions, idle machines and full containers. Click a row to go where you can deal with it. "Your seedlings" lists your Seedlings and what each is doing. Click one to open their page in your society's Members section.

#### Browsing and Comparing Organizations
<!-- keywords: all businesses all societies list table compare sort grid column search other businesses public listed -->
*Listing all organizations of a type, the comparison table, and the search in the top bar.*
Select a type's tile. The switch at the top of the list beside the tiles changes between your own organizations and all listed ones, for example "My Businesses" and "All Businesses". The All list has a Search field. The grid button beside it opens a table that compares the organizations, with yours first. The sort button next to the Search field changes how the table is sorted.
- Societies: island, tier, Seedlings against the society's limit, Cultivators online, open positions with their wage range, and claimable plots. Societies from every island are listed. A lock marks a private society.
- Businesses: employees, open positions with their wage range, and the balance where you may see it.
- Groups: members, and the balance where you may see it.
- Departments: members, open positions and balance. My Departments and All Departments show your own departments and the departments that your society role lets you manage.

The search field in the window's top bar searches the organization you have selected. Its name shows in the field. Click the × next to the name to search the whole window, including other societies and open jobs.

#### Viewing Another Society in the Organizations Window
<!-- keywords: other society look at visit migrate here rocket button greyed out grayed can't migrate private password trade distance -->
*What another society's page shows, and the Migrate here button.*
Open a society you are not in from All Societies. Its Overview shows its announcement, tier, island, population and average mood. It also shows whether it is public or private, its claimable plots, who is online, its open positions with their wage range, and its wealth. You also see the distance to your society and what your two societies sold each other. You can also read a public society's Constitution. Choosing a new constitution and the confidence vote happen only on your own society's page.

The rocket button next to the society's name, Migrate here, opens the migration window with that society as the destination. When you cannot move there, it is greyed out and its tooltip names the reason:
- "This society is full"
- "This society has no claimable plots for new members"
- "This society banned you"
- "You can't migrate to this island right now"
- "You need a seedling to migrate"
- "Migration is temporarily disabled, try again later."

A private society shows outsiders only its population and tier. The migration window asks for its password.

#### An Organization's Pages
<!-- keywords: sections overview finance ballots goals policies constitution moderation production locations locate map pin rename pencil not in your properties members only non member jobs join apply -->
*The sections of an organization's page, its Overview, and what non-members see.*
Select an organization to open its page. Its sections are listed under its name in the list beside the tiles. Every organization you belong to has Overview, Roles and Members. A business calls its Roles and Members pages Jobs and Employees.
Finance shows when you have the See finances permission. The default member roles of a society have it. Ballots shows when the organization has a vote or an elected role. Your society also has Goals, Policies and Constitution. Moderation shows to those allowed to banish members. An organization whose buildings have production machines has Production.

The Overview names the organization's type and society and has a chat button. Its figures include the balance, the members, open positions and daily wages, items for sale, and contracts. Click Open positions to see the jobs that are hiring. Click Contracts to open them in the Properties Panel. A Needs attention card shows what waits on you there.

The Locations card lists its buildings and plots. Click one to open it in the Properties Panel. Click its map pin, Locate, to move the camera to it. A location you cannot open from here is dimmed. "Not in your Properties" means you have no permission to view it in the Properties Panel. "Members only" means only members can open it. "Products and services" lists what it sells. If your role may rename the organization, click the pencil next to its name.

An organization you are not a member of shows its Overview. When it is hiring, it also shows a Jobs table with each job's workplace, required skills and wage. A job marked "Hires at once" has a Join button. A job marked "Hires by application" has an Apply button. You can take an application back with Cancel application.

#### Members Page in the Organizations Window
<!-- keywords: members employees list who is online now filter paid remove from role kick leave organizations tab member organizations businesses in society cultivator -->
*The Members (or Employees) page: filters, the Organizations tab, and leaving or removing.*
The Members page lists every Seedling with a role in the organization, with their Cultivator, roles and wage. A business calls it Employees. Open Filters to show only some roles or workplaces. Its Status filter shows members whose Cultivator is online ("Online now") or members who earn a wage ("Paid"). Sort by Name, Last online, Role or Wage.

Organizations can be members of an organization too. For example, businesses are members of their society. When an organization has both kinds of member, the page has two tabs, Seedlings and Organizations. The Organizations tab lists the member organizations with their role and member count.

Click a member to open their page. It shows their Cultivator, their roles there, the other organizations they are in, their skills, and how your Seedlings know them. Click "Schedule time together" to plan a meeting with them.

Click Leave to take one of your own Seedlings out of the organization. They lose the access it gives. Click Remove to take a role away from another Seedling, when you may assign that role. Removing a Seedling from a job fires them. An elected role cannot be removed here.

#### Roles Page in the Organizations Window
<!-- keywords: roles page jobs page filter hiring vacant elected appointed assign offer role invite plus button branch location new role new job card add duplicate copy starting point -->
*How the Roles (or Jobs) page is laid out, and assigning or offering a role from it.*
The Roles page groups an organization's roles by workplace. A business calls it Jobs. Roles with no workplace come first, then one group per location. Open Filters to show one Seedling's roles, or only roles that are Hiring, Elected, Appointed, Vacant or Paid.
Click "New" to create a role. For a society, department or business, it offers starting points such as Hired Job or Elected Official, and Custom role for an empty role. "Add" next to a building's heading creates a job at that building. It shows only when the organization hires and administers the building, and you may create roles. "Duplicate" in a role card's menu opens a copy of the role, which you can change before you create it. "Open location" opens the location in the Properties Panel.

Each role card has buttons for what you may do with it:
- "+" assigns the role to a Seedling. It shows on an appointed role, and only to those who may appoint it.
- The envelope offers the role to a Seedling, for a role filled by invitation.
- The inbox button shows how many applications wait, and opens them in the Inbox.

#### Organizations Inbox and Outbox
<!-- keywords: inbox outbox envelope top bar application applicant review hire reject accept decline invitation offer withdraw take back expire where accept job applicants role offer residence invite -->
*The inbox of the Organizations window: invitations, job applications, and what you sent.*
The inbox of the Organizations window holds invitations and job applications. Buildings have their own Inbox, which holds items. The inbox icon sits in the window's top bar, next to PAM. Its badge counts what waits on your answer.

The Inbox tab has two groups:
- Invitations: role offers, group invitations and residence invitations for your Seedlings. Each one names the organization, your Seedling it is for, the role offered and who sent it. It also shows a home when one comes with it, and when it expires. Click Accept or Decline.
- Applications: Seedlings applying for jobs you may hire for. Applicants who meet every requirement come first, with a tick. Click Hire or Reject. Hire is greyed out when the applicant misses a requirement, no position is open, the job is closed, or the applicant is gone. Point at it to see why.

The Outbox tab holds what you sent: "Offers you sent" and "Applications you sent". Click Withdraw to take one back.

#### Founding and Disbanding Organizations
<!-- keywords: create found new business group department plus button greyed out grayed cost limit disband close delete dissolve shut down end business group department -->
*Founding a business, group or department with the plus tile, and disbanding one.*
Click the plus tile, New Organization, at the bottom left of the Organizations window. Pick the type, such as Business, Group or Department. Give it a name and pick which of your Seedlings founds it. A department has no founder. You set up its founding role instead (see Departments). The dialog shows the founding cost when there is one. A type you cannot found is greyed out. Its tooltip names the reason:
- You do not have the permission to found it.
- "Only one Business per player, and you already have one."
- Your Seedlings have reached their limit for that type.
- Your society has reached its limit for that type.

Disband is in the More menu (⋮) on the organization's Overview, for those allowed to dissolve it. A society cannot be disbanded. The dialog says what will happen:
- Members lose the access it gives.
- Everyone with a wage there leaves without severance.
- A business's remaining buildings pass to the society.

Type the organization's name to confirm. Disbanding cannot be undone.

Disband is greyed out while something blocks it. Point at it to see why:
- "Members who can't disband it must leave first" shows on a group. Every member whose role may not disband the group leaves first.
- "Its buildings must be handed over first" shows on a group or department. Give every building it owns or administers to someone else first, for example with Change Owner on the building's page in the Properties Panel.

*Creating a business account and branches, plus the ways a business earns money.*

(May not apply: organization browser enabled.) Business management lives in the Organizations window (see The Organizations Window section): your business appears under the Businesses tile on the left of the window. (Alternative; may not apply.) The Business Panel allows you to create and manage a business. Each Cultivator can own only one business account, but that business can have multiple Business Branches, each tied to its own building. If you want to run more than one line of work, add another Business Branch to your existing business rather than trying to create a second business account. Creating a business requires holding a role with the Found organizations permission for businesses. The default Adult role gets it once the society reaches the Adv. Camp tier, and the Mayor can give it to custom roles. After the society adopts a constitution, its adult role, such as Citizen, Subject or Anarchist, has it. You need this permission only to create a business. The Seedling that owns a business manages it as its Owner. When your Seedling's role grants that permission, you can create a business account, which gives you a wallet shared across all your business branches. To set up a Business Branch, acquire a building from the Real Estate Market, or use the Land Planner to construct a new one on an empty plot. Just make sure to use your business wallet to complete the transaction. The new building serves as a Business Branch, providing you with an inbox and outbox for deliveries(May not apply: !organization browser enabled.) , and granting access to all business management tools, including Security, Employment, Production, and more.

To create the business account: (May not apply: organization browser enabled.) open the Organizations window, click the plus tile (New Organization) at the bottom left of the Organizations window and pick "Business". If Business is greyed out, see Founding and Disbanding Organizations for the reason. Then (Alternative; may not apply.) open the Business Panel and click "Create Account", choose which of your Seedlings owns the business, pick a name, and pay the creation fee from that Seedling's wallet (the fee goes to the society's treasury). The business wallet is separate from your Seedling's personal wallet. (May not apply: organization browser enabled.) Each Business Branch opens in the Properties Panel like any other building you manage. In the Properties Panel's list, the branch has Deliveries, Market, Services and Contracts under it. Its storage and machines are in the Properties Panel too. The business's Production section in the Organizations window gives an overview of its production. (Alternative; may not apply.) A branch adds these tabs to the Business Panel: Overview, Employment, Production, Services, Inventory, Contracts, and Deliveries.

(May not apply: organization browser enabled.) Your business's own page in the Organizations window shows its key figures, and its Finance section shows the business wallet: balance charts, a searchable ledger of all its transactions, and a Make a Transfer button to send SeedCoins from the business wallet to any recipient. To disband the business, open the More menu (⋮) on its Overview and choose Disband (see Founding and Disbanding Organizations). The money left in its wallet goes to the Seedlings whose role may disband the business. By default, that is only the Owner. To hand the business to another of your Seedlings, open the More menu (⋮) on its Overview and choose Change Owner. (Alternative; may not apply.) If none of your Seedlings is left there, the business is dissolved and the money in its wallet goes to the society.

- Hire employees and run production, then sell the goods (see Employment Management, Crafting and Production Tasks, and Selling on the Marketplace).
- Run a service location like a cafe, offering advertised services that visiting Seedlings pay for, plus an optional entry fee (see Services).
- Deliver items for other Cultivators and get paid per distance (see Deliveries).
- Trade on the Marketplace with Sell Orders, Buy Orders, and Contracts (see Buying on the Marketplace, Selling on the Marketplace, and Contracts).
- Own plots and buildings, rent them out, or resell them (see Real Estate Market).

#### Business Partners
<!-- keywords: partner co-owner co owner second owner share business together invite partner add owner how many partners limit seats -->
*The Partner role every business has: who can be a partner and what partners can do.*
Every business has a Partner role, for running the business together with the Owner. The Owner offers it to a Seedling with the envelope button on the Partner card on the business's Jobs page. The Seedling becomes a partner by accepting the offer in the inbox of the Organizations window. The offer dialog shows how many Partner seats are taken.
Partners can do most of what the Owner can do, such as managing jobs, buildings and production. By default, partners can see the business's finances and spend its money. By default, only the Owner can transfer its money, sell the business's buildings and plots or give them away, disband the business, delete roles, and change the Owner and Partner roles. The notifications about the business's buildings, plots and rentals, such as unpaid rent, go to the roles that can sell them. By default, that is only the Owner. Partners get none of the business's SeedCoin when it is disbanded. The Owner can change the Partner role's permissions like any other role's, or delete it.


### Real Estate Market
*Buying, selling, and renting plots and buildings through property listings.*

The Real Estate Market is the listings app for buying, selling, and renting property within your society. It shows the plots and buildings currently available, including anything that's been listed for sale plus buildings put up for rent.

To purchase, use the wallet of whoever should own the property. For example, use a Seedling's personal wallet for a privately owned building, a business wallet for a Business Branch, and so on. Ownership transfers to the wallet's owner on purchase. If you need to change ownership later, you can do that from the Properties Panel.

Listings themselves are created from the Properties Panel. That's where you put a building up for sale or rent, and where you list a plot for sale. Note that plots can only be sold, not rented. Renting is buildings-only.

### Land Planner
*Editing plots, placing roads, expanding territory, and plot operations like split and merge.*

The Land Planner is the spatial planning app for land. From here you can edit any plot you have permission to: a plot you own, or one your role gives you rights over (such as a business owner managing a business-owned plot, or a Housing Manager working with society plots).

Use the tools in the bottom-left corner to switch between the modes for shaping your land. Select the plot you want to edit to access Farming and Build Mode for it. Switch to Road mode to place down new roads, which cost SeedCoin. Switch to Territory mode to see all the land your society controls; with the right permission you can buy adjacent territory there with SeedCoin to expand it.

If your society role grants the right permissions, you can also build major society structures like Subways and the Spaceport from the Land Planner. The tools for building Subways and the Spaceport are hidden for Seedlings without the right permission.

#### Managing Plots
<!-- keywords: migrate, migrating, migration, moving societies, seize, take plot, confiscate -->
Each operation below is reached in two ways: select a plot in the Land Planner's Plot Mode (an overview of all plots you manage), or click a plot in the game view to open its context menu. Most of them work on any plot you manage, and none of them cost SeedCoin.

**Split**: Divide one of your plots into several smaller ones. Choose "Split", then drag across the plot to draw the new boundaries; you can split the same plot multiple times. Each new plot keeps the original owner. You can't split through a building: every building must end up fully inside one of the new plots.

**Merge**: Combine two or more of your adjacent plots into a single larger one. Choose "Merge", then hold Ctrl or Shift to select the plots and confirm. They must all share the same owner, sit on the same territory, and fit together into a rectangle (no L-shapes or gaps). You can't merge a Seedling-owned plot with a society-owned one, even if you have the permission. Any buildings on the plots are kept.

**Set as Claimable Plot**: Turn an empty plot into one another Seedling can claim. The plot must be empty first: no buildings, no objects, and not rented or listed for rent. Setting a plot claimable requires a role with the Edit plots permission (society land management, held by roles like Urban Minister, Housing Manager, or President/Prime Minister). Once it's claimable, any society member allowed to claim land (new Cultivators get this when their Seedling arrives) can claim it by placing a tent on it; when the tent is built, they own the plot. Societies use this to hand free starter land to newcomers. You can switch it back off as long as no one has claimed it yet.

Claiming this way is a one-time, permanent action per Seedling within a society: once a Seedling has claimed a plot in a society, it can't claim another one there again, even after deleting that plot. A Seedling who wants a different plot in the same society has to buy or rent one on the Real Estate Market instead. Migrating to a different society is the exception: it resets this, so the Seedling can claim a free plot again in their new society.

**Delete plot**: Permanently remove one of your plots. The land returns to the society's territory: it's no longer a plot and can no longer be privately owned, though Seedlings can still harvest natural resources there like anywhere in the territory. Once confirmed, the plot can't be recovered. A plot with buildings on it can't be deleted, and neither can a plot someone is currently claiming. If the plot was listed on the Real Estate Market, that listing is removed automatically. Deleting a plot you claimed for free does not let you claim another one in the same society — see the note above.

**Seize plot**: The society takes a plot away from its owner. The owner gets nothing back. You need a role with the "Seize buildings and plots" permission, such as the Housing Manager, Urban Minister, Autarch, Prime Minister or President. To take a building, use Seize building in the building's menu.

**Draw new plot**: This option only appears if your society role grants permission to shape territory (such as a Housing Manager or Urban Minister); most Cultivators won't see it. With it, Plot Mode has a Draw mode for creating a brand-new plot on bare territory: choose "Draw new plot", then drag across an empty area of the territory to set the plot's boundaries. The plot must stay within the society's territory, can't overlap an existing plot, and can't be drawn across a building.

### Build Mode
<!-- keywords: floor room edit building nicer decorate blueprint blue construction built -->
*Designing new buildings, entering Build Mode, construction materials, and cancelling construction.*

Build Mode allows you to construct entirely new buildings on empty plots or modify existing ones. To enter Build Mode, your Seedling needs the right to build on that plot: either by owning it personally, or by holding a role (such as Housing Manager) that the owning organization has granted the authority to build there. On society-owned plots, building is typically reserved for the Housing Manager, the Mayor, or a custom role the Mayor has granted that authority to.

#### How to Enter Build Mode
You can enter Build Mode in several ways:
1. Click on an owned plot and select "Create New Building".
2. Click on an owned building and select "Build Mode".
3. Open the Land Planner from the right sidebar and select the plot you want to build on. Clicking the construction icon will take you to Build Mode.
4. Use the Properties Panel(May not apply: !organization browser enabled.)  or Business Panel: select the plot or building you want to modify, then click "Open Builder" for a plot or "Edit in Builder" for a building.

#### Editing Building Design
<!-- keywords: delete wall remove block erase undo -->
As a Cultivator, you take on the role of a building designer, responsible for the layout and structure of the space. Seedlings will take over the construction part.
Once in Build Mode, you’ll have access to a variety of construction tools to place walls, floors, doors, windows, elevators, and more. You can also customize lighting and room layouts.
Use the bottom-left menu to select the elements you want to build or customize. The bottom-middle panel shows a preview of the material or object. The bottom-right panel lists the materials needed for your design.
To remove something you built, pick the tool you placed it with. Hold Ctrl (Delete Mode) and click it. This works for walls, floors, windows, entrances, doors, elevators and Room Blocks. There is no Undo button. Cancel throws away all changes you haven't confirmed. Pieces you remove before confirming are also taken off the materials list.
Outdoor Block, found in the Exterior tab next to Structure Block, marks open ground next to the building as part of it: a yard or garden. Anything placed on Outdoor Block tiles, including machines, storage, and farm fields, counts as inside the building for jobs and access while staying outdoors, so it is the way to bring outdoor equipment within reach of a Business Branch's employees (see Employment Management).
Important: Every floor needs proper access: higher floors require elevators.
For example, in a building with three floors, you’ll need an elevator from the ground floor to the first floor, and another from the first to the second floor. Additionally, your building must have at least one main entrance to be accessible. You'll receive notifications (above the right UI panel) indicating whether your design is ready to complete.
When finished, click "Confirm Building Design". A blue hologram of your building will appear, awaiting delivery of the required materials to the inbox to begin construction.

#### Construction Process
Buildings cost materials, which vary depending on how you designed them. Selecting the blueprint opens a panel listing the materials needed. Once the building blueprint is confirmed, the required materials must be delivered to the construction site inbox. You can either purchase them from the Marketplace or move them from another inventory.
You can use Direct Control to assign your Seedling to construct your building design, making them start immediately. Or open the Scheduler and add the "Build" priority. Buildings under construction are marked with a blue ring. You can hover or click on the site to see the current progress and check for any missing materials.
You don't need every material before building starts. Work begins once the site has at least one of each material it still needs, and goes on as more arrives. When a material runs out, building waits for the next delivery. A site marked "Blocked" is missing a material, or, near completion, has an object placed in an invalid spot.
If you are constructing a business building and your business already has many employees, you can also use job tasks to assign employees to do the construction.
Building trains your Seedling's Construction skill, plus the skill for the building's exterior style, for example Bricklaying for a Brick building. Build Mode lists the skill levels a design will need next to the materials, but these levels are not required yet. For now, any Seedling can build any design and haul its materials. The Skills tab in Focus Mode shows the blocks and styles each skill unlocks under "New Unlocks".
Each exterior style has its own skill, which Construction unlocks at a set level. The Skills Reference lists how many blocks and floors a style skill allows at each level. Those lower limits are not enforced yet: for now, a design in any style can be as big as that style's level 50 limit, and no bigger. Outdoor blocks don't count.

Construction makes a Seedling build 2.5% faster per level (+125% at level 50). Outdoor blocks train it 1.2× and balconies 1.5×, and building in Wooden Shack, Wooden Planks, Brick, Plaxin or High Tech multiplies that by 1.2, 1.4, 1.6, 1.8 or 2.

#### Cancel Construction
You can cancel a building’s construction at any time. However, be aware that any materials already moved to the inbox will be permanently lost, so proceed with caution.
If you’re only editing an existing building, the materials will remain in your inbox. However, any materials that have already been used in the construction process will not be returned.

#### Destroying a Building
<!-- keywords: demolish tear down remove building delete building get rid of building knock down refund materials back get resources back destroy button greyed out inbox outbox -->
*The steps to destroy a finished building, where the Destroy button is, and what you get back.*
Destroying a building gives nothing back. Every material that went into it is lost.
Follow these steps in order:
1. Empty the building. Pack or delete every placed object except the inbox and outbox. Objects in poor condition cannot be packed, so repair or delete them. Then move everything out of the inbox and outbox too.
2. Shrink the building in Build Mode. Remove everything until only the inbox, the outbox and an entrance are left, then click "Confirm Building Design". Wait until your Seedlings finish that construction.
3. Open the [PropertiesApp] and select the building. In the "Modify Building" block, click "Destroy", then confirm.
Deleting blocks in Build Mode does not destroy the building, and the blocks holding the inbox and outbox cannot be deleted.
You can't destroy a building while a construction is going on. It is also not possible until every step above is done. Hover the greyed-out Destroy button to see which step is missing.
Anyone who can build or modify the building can destroy it too.

### Homes vs Business Branches
*The two player building types and how to acquire each.*

There are two types of player created buildings. Homes and Business Branches.
Homes are buildings owned by a Seedling, i.e. Seedling residences. The first building your Seedling owns or rents is automatically set as their home. If they have other owned or rented buildings available, you can switch their residence to one of them from the Properties Panel.
Business Branches are player-created buildings owned by your business where services and production and sales can occur. Make sure to start a business before trying to build a branch. You can hire Seedlings as employee once you have Business Branches. Business Branches have advanced building management tools (Employment, Services, Crafting, etc.) compared to Homes.
Businesses are organizations too. Their day-to-day structure is the Owner plus jobs: you direct employees through the jobs you create - their tasks, wage, schedule, and required skills. (May not apply: organization browser enabled.) The business's Jobs page in the Organizations window lists all its roles, jobs included; there the Owner and Partners can create additional custom roles and pick the permissions they grant. (Alternative; may not apply.) A job's permissions are fixed by the game (the access its work needs, such as its workplace) and businesses have no role editor: what an employee does is shaped through the job's tasks and schedule, and everything else stays with the owner.

#### How to Acquire a Home
To find a home for your Seedling, browse the Real Estate Market to see if there are existing buildings for sale or rent. If you purchase a building using your Seedling's personal wallet, your Seedling becomes the official owner. Alternatively, you can buy an empty plot from the Real Estate Market and construct a building from scratch. You can also rent a building from another Cultivator. The first building your Seedling owns or rents is automatically set as their home, so once the purchase or rental goes through they'll have a place to go when you schedule "rest at home" time in their Calendar. Having a home of their own also gives them the privacy and comfort of their own space.
If you're worried about your Seedling getting lonely, you can invite other Seedlings to live with you! Just remember: anyone with access to the building also has access to its inventories. But hey, sharing is caring!

#### How to get Business Branches
The fastest way to acquire a Business Branch is to use your Business Wallet to purchase a plot or building from the Real Estate Market. If you bought a plot, you can construct a building from scratch, but it might take a bit longer. You can also rent a building using your Business Wallet, in which case the business becomes the administrator of the rented building.
If your Seedling already owns a plot or building, you can simply reassign ownership to your Business. Select it in the Properties Panel, click Change Owner and pick the business.

### Renting Buildings
<!-- keywords: rent out house landlord tenant -->
*Renting buildings out or from others, rent payments, and ending rentals.*

Buildings can be rented from other Cultivators. Only buildings can be rented; plots cannot be rented. Renting a building requires holding a role that grants the right to rent property. Adults gain that right as a Society tier reward. The Mayor can also grant it to custom roles. Groups and businesses can rent as soon as they are founded.

#### Renting Out a Building
Only businesses, groups, departments and the Society can rent out buildings. A business's Owner or Partner can rent out a building the business owns. A Group Head can rent out a building the group owns. A department's Leader can rent out a building the department owns. Roles that manage society property, such as the Housing Manager or Urban Minister, can rent out society-owned buildings. A Seedling cannot put a building it owns itself up for rent. Create a Rent Listing through the [PropertiesApp]; the option appears once your Society reaches Tier 4. Set a daily rent price in SeedCoin. A building cannot be listed for both rent and sale at the same time.

While a building is rented out, the tenant runs it day to day. The tenant controls its storage, its furniture and other objects, including moving or deleting them, and its deliveries. The landlord can no longer do these things. The landlord keeps only the owner's rights: managing and ending the rental, construction on the building, changing its plot boundaries, and selling or transferring it.

#### Renting a Building
To rent a building, browse the available rent listings and select one. The wallet you use to pay determines who becomes the administrator of the building: for example, using your Seedling's personal wallet makes your Seedling the administrator, while using a business wallet makes the business the administrator. If you want your Seedling to live in the rented building, don't forget to assign them as a resident after renting. Rent is paid automatically every tau in SeedCoin. If a Seedling renting with their own wallet cannot pay, they become a Criminal for a while (see The Criminal Role). The Seedling or organization paying the rent must hold the right to rent property in your society.

#### Ending a Rental
Either the landlord or the tenant can terminate a rental agreement. Termination requires a 3-tau notice period, during which the tenant continues paying rent and retains access to the building. After the notice period ends, administration returns to the original owner. If the tenant misses a rent payment, the landlord can immediately evict them without the notice period.

When a rental ends, all furniture and placed objects remain in the building: they are not destroyed or removed. In case there are residents in the building, all residents are removed from the building. In case the rented building is used for a business, all employees are fired, all job postings are cleared, all market orders (buy and sell) are cancelled, all contracts are force-terminated, the service location is closed, and all room access controls are reset to public.

### Rooms
<!-- keywords: room aesthetic nicer, room stats score -->
*Room structure and scores: sound, cleanliness, aesthetics, and lighting effects on Seedlings.*

All buildings may consist of rooms. Every floor is considered one room by default. To split a floor into rooms, open the Interior tab in Build Mode and drag with the Room Block tool. To remove a wall between rooms, hold Ctrl and click its Room Block. To see how good a room is, select the building, open the Rooms list in its context menu and click the room. Room Effects shows its sound, aesthetics, cleanliness and light. As a Cultivator, you should ensure your Seedlings spend time in rooms with positive effects to keep them happy and healthy.

#### Sounds
Objects in a room can emit sound thereby influencing a Seedling’s Activities and Mood. Rooms have two main indicators for sound: Volume and Harmony
Volume has three thresholds: Loud, Moderate and Low.
A room can either be Harmonious (positive sounding like ambience or music) or Dissonant (negative sounding like machines).
If Seedlings seem annoyed or happy, look at their thoughts to understand why and you can inspect the building and look at a room's sound score which lets you know the current status of a room and all the objects that contribute to the sound score. If loud machinery is making your Seedlings go crazy, make sure to have break rooms with a harmonious environment to help them chill out for a while.
A Loud room also blocks activities outright. A Seedling cannot rest, recover energy, or study there at all, and will only use it in a crisis. The bed's context menu warns you when its room is too noisy.

#### Cleanliness
Every room has a cleanliness score that ranges from Clean to Acceptable, Dirty, and finally Filthy. Keeping a room Clean provides positive boosts to Seedlings, while Dirty or Filthy rooms will cause negative effects. Dirt accumulates naturally every tau, but certain events (like a Seedling getting sick or having an accident) will create dirt much faster. Seedlings will take action to clean a room when:
- Priorities: If they have been assigned "Cleaning" in their Scheduler.
- Emergency: If a room reaches the "Filthy" stage, they will prioritize cleaning it.
- Direct Control: If you manually order a Seedling to clean a specific "dirt patch" within the room.

#### Aesthetics
A room’s aesthetic score measures its beauty and ranges from Neutral to Cozy and Lovely. High aesthetic scores improve the living conditions for Seedlings spending time in those spaces.
You can influence this score through your interior design choices:
- Decorating: Adding beautiful furniture increases the score.
- Placing heavy machinery reduces the aesthetic score, though a room’s aesthetics will never drop below zero.
- Furniture Sets: To optimize your rooms, use items from the same Furniture Set. Matching pieces provide a multiplier bonus to the aesthetic score, rewarding you for creating a cohesive and well-designed environment.

#### Lighting
Light affects your Seedlings' Stats, mood and how well they do activities. Light levels go from Darkness to Low Light, Medium and Bright. Keep bedrooms dark: Medium light or brighter wakes sleeping Seedlings. The bed's context menu warns you when its room is too bright.

Indoors, each room has one Average Light score that counts for the whole room. Windows and lights raise it. Bigger rooms need more of them. Windows only give light from 6:00 to 20:00, so add lamps or candles for the night. Curtains don't change the light.

Outdoors, the sun lights everything from 6:00 to 20:00. At night, a Seedling only gets light within range of a light such as a streetlamp. Balconies count as outdoors.

Every light has a Light Mode in its context menu: On, Off, Day Only or Night Only. It only shines while its mode is active.

A Seedling in a dark room, or outdoors at night with no light nearby, gets the "In Darkness" status effect. It lowers Dexterity by 25% and Perception by 50%, so they fail activities more often and can get hurt.

To fix a dark spot:
- Indoors, add windows or lights until the room's Average Light is above Darkness.
- If a room is dark at night, check its lights aren't set to Off or Day Only.
- Outdoors, place streetlamps so every spot your Seedlings work at night is in range of one.

#### Firing an Employee

*What firing an employee costs, and when no severance is paid.*
Firing an employee pays them severance worth one shift's wage for the job they held, taken from the business wallet as soon as you fire them. If the wage is 0, no severance is paid. #### Production Chain & Employment Tasks
To optimize your production flow, you must think through every step a Seedling must take to get from raw material to a finished product. Your role as Cultivator is to design this system and set up the employee tasks, while your Seedlings carry them out. Remember, things don't move around by themselves; each step requires a Seedling's engagement.

##### The Three Stages of Production
An efficient production chain typically involves three stages:
1. Resource Collection: Seedlings gather raw materials (like wood or stone) from the world. Alternatively, you can buy materials from the Marketplace.
2. Hauling & Processing: Seedlings move these materials from a storage location, and then operate machines to process them into finished goods.
3. Output & Delivery: Seedlings move the finished goods from the machine's output to another storage container, ready for use or sale.

##### Assigning Production Tasks
To initiate production, recruit Seedlings to work for you and assign them with proper job task priorities using the Employment Management tool(May not apply: organization browser enabled.)  on the business's Jobs page in the Organizations window (Alternative; may not apply.)  in the Business Panel:
1. Select the activity type you want them to prioritize, such as "Produce."
2. Specify the object or item of that activity, for example, "Produce Algae paste."

Once this is set as the highest priority, the assigned Seedling will only do this and nothing else. However, if for any reason they cannot carry out the highest priority (like not having enough materials for the production), they will move on to their second task. Keep your production chain design and employment task priority system in mind to ensure efficiency.

##### Preventing Work Interruption
Even with a job assigned, Seedlings may not always prioritize your assignment if they have an urgent need (such as hunger or thirst). To prevent this interruption, consider providing facilities for food and water near their workplace so they can take care of themselves quickly and not walk away from their jobs for extended periods. Additionally, if none of the given priorities are possible for some reason, Seedlings may also just hang around at their workplace. To ensure continuous work, make sure that: The necessary machines are turned on; All required materials are available; Production tasks are set on machines; The right amount of machines are present so Seedlings are not occupied all the time waiting to use equipment. Observe your employees and how they work; this is the best way to determine how to manage their jobs effectively.

##### Employees Only Work Inside the Branch
<!-- keywords: employees ignore outdoor machine storage field outside building won't work on plot yard -->
A job is always located at its Business Branch building, and an employee on the job only picks up work that counts as inside that building: its rooms and any ground you have marked with Outdoor Block in Build Mode. A machine, container, farm field, or resource that merely stands on the same plot outside those tiles is out of the job's reach, and the employee will not touch it. To bring outdoor equipment into the job, extend the building over it: in Build Mode, choose Building Block, place Outdoor Block over the ground where the equipment stands, and keep the objects on those tiles. Setting a Calendar block's location to a whole plot is a setting of your own Seedling's schedule only; a job has no such option.

##### Setting harvesting as a job task
It is possible to give an employee a job task priority to harvest resources, with two conditions. The resource must count as inside the branch building, which in practice means it stands on the building's Outdoor Block tiles; a resource elsewhere on the plot is out of the job's reach. And a resource growing in the world must first be saved for later ("Add to TODO list" on it) before a scheduled harvest priority can select it; when that is missing, the schedule status names it as the reason.

### Crafting and Production Tasks
<!-- keywords: make craft bed item -->
*Crafting items with machines: production tasks, limits, and the Crafting management tool.*

Crafting items on Avesta is a key way to progress. While your Seedlings can craft anywhere using Direct Control, they must have access to a compatible crafting machine (like a Crafting Table) and the necessary materials.

#### The Crafting Process
1. Preparation: Use the Catalog to determine what you can craft. The catalog shows you which materials, machines, and skills are needed. Some items also require a Schematic, which is an extra set of crafting instructions that a Seedling must learn.
2. Create Production Task: Once you have the materials, place a crafting machine in a building. Click on the machine to bring up the menu and select "Production" to create a Production Task for the desired product. Once a production task exists on the machine, its context menu gains a new direct control option to send your Seedling to work on it. For a quick one-off job, pick "Quick Produce" from the machine's menu instead: it creates the task and immediately sends your selected Seedling to carry it out. As a rule of thumb: use "Quick Produce" when you want something made fast, and set up machine tasks combined with a "Run Machine" priority in the Scheduler when you want production to run automatically. If an option in the machine's menu is greyed out, hover over it and it will tell you what's missing. A Schematic your Seedling hasn't learned is the usual reason, and the Skills tab in Focus Mode shows you how to get it.
3. Send your Seedling to pick up the task: Every action on Avesta needs to be completed by a Seedling. After creating the Production Task, assign your Seedling to do it. If not, another Seedling nearby may pick up the task as well.

#### Production Limits
When creating a Production Task, you can set stop conditions: "Do X times" (the machine runs the process a set number of times) or "Do until X in building storage" (the machine stops once enough of that item is available in the building's storage). A "Do until" task doesn't end. When stock falls below X again, Seedlings start making more on their own. A machine placed outdoors on a plot counts the plot's outdoor storage instead. If you see "production limit reached," it means the stop condition you set has been met and the machine has stopped producing. To resolve this, click on the machine and modify the production task conditions: you can increase the limit, remove it, or set it to "Do forever" for continuous production.

You can also add conditions to a task with Add Condition, for example "less than 10 Wood Plank in building storage". They are listed under "While". The task only runs while all of them are true. The message "Configured storage conditions are not met" means one of them isn't true right now. Wait for the storage to change, or edit or remove the condition.

Setting up a production task on machines is necessary for a Seedling to be able to work on it. Your role as Cultivator is to instruct what to do. Without your hint, Seedlings won't invent tasks and pick up things to do by themselves. Therefore, even if a machine has materials and is turned on, you must use the machine's interface to queue a production task before a Seedling will recognize that work is available for them to perform.

#### Advanced Crafting Management Tool
For more complex production, use the Crafting management tool. (May not apply: organization browser enabled.) This tool is the business's Production section in the Organizations window. Each machine is also listed under Machines in the Properties Panel. (Alternative; may not apply.) This tool is available in your Business Branch and also appears in the Properties Panel. Use it to create a new task for a specific machine, detailing what you want to craft. The management tool is particularly useful for large scale production, which often require multiple machines and intermediate steps. It allows you to have an overview on multiple machines. Once a task is created, a Seedling must be assigned to it, and they will begin work as long as all required inputs are present.
Crafting tasks run on machines. For gathering resources, see Harvesting or Setting harvesting as a job task.
(May not apply: organization browser enabled.) The Production section appears once one of the organization's buildings has a production machine. (Alternative; may not apply.) The message "Production overview not available" in a Business Branch's Production view means the branch has no crafting machine and hasn't made anything recently. To start, place a machine in the building, give it a task and assign an employee.

### Building Inventory and Storage
<!-- keywords: place furniture move object around unpack packed arrow placement furnish -->
*Storage containers, inventory management, Placement Mode, Furnish Mode, Inbox, and Outbox.*

Many objects, like fridges and shelves, serve as storage containers in Avesta. In an early society, the Kernel is the main inventory, letting you store resources and grab food/water for basic needs.
- Click on any inventory to see its contents.
- Hover over an item for a brief description.
- If an item is placeable, clicking it will take you directly into Placement Mode, allowing you to set it down within your building's boundaries.

#### Advanced Inventory Management
For more advanced inventory management, head to the dedicated storage view of any building you own: open the Properties Panel, select the building, then select Storage(May not apply: !organization browser enabled.)  — or open the Business Panel and go to its Inventory tab. The view shows all items stored across all containers of whatever you have selected (a single building, or your whole property tree when nothing is selected), including Inboxes, Outboxes, Fridges, Cabinets, and more.
This view allows you to:
- Set Delivery Priority: In a container's Settings, assign it Low, Medium, or High priority. Seedlings move items to the container with the higher priority, so this decides where goods pile up. It applies to the whole container, not to individual item types. An Outbox is fixed at the highest priority and cannot be changed.
- Choose Accepted Items: A container's Settings also has an "Items and categories" tree, separate from its priority. It is how a fridge ends up holding only perishables. Tick a category and the container accepts everything under it. Click a single item inside a ticked category to exclude just that one. Accept all and Accept none set everything at once, and each accepted item can take an amount or Unlimited. You can copy one container's settings and paste them onto another. You can only pick or exclude a limited number of items individually, so use a category for anything broader. A new container accepts only what it is made for. A fridge takes meals and drinks, a dresser takes clothing, and a nightstand takes remedies. Plain storage accepts nothing until you tick what it should hold. This covers Storage Platforms, Warehouse Shelves, the Kernel and a building's Inbox. An empty Inbox stays free for incoming deliveries. A container placed a while ago may still accept everything.
- Inspect Item Details: View all known information about an item, including its name, volume, nutrition, and time to decay.
- Transfer Items: Select an item and click Transfer to move it to another container, as described in Transferring Items Between Containers.
- List Items for Sale: If the building is a Business Branch, you can quickly create a Sell Order for an item.
- Access Placement Mode: When inspecting placeable objects, you can activate Placement Mode to set them down anywhere within the building's bounds.

#### Transferring Items Between Containers
<!-- keywords: move item another container building send items transfer route drag drop haul cancel stop transfer to same location another location -->
*Moving items to another container with the Transfer button or drag-and-drop, and cancelling a transfer.*

Open the building that holds the items in the Properties Panel and select Storage(May not apply: !organization browser enabled.) , or in the Business Panel and go to its Inventory tab. Select an item and click Transfer. Shift-click to select several items first. You can only transfer items when you have permission to both the source and the destination container. The Transfer button offers two choices.

"To same location" moves items between containers in the same building, or between containers on the same plot outside any building. A building is a separate location from its plot and from other buildings on that plot. Pick the Destination, set how many of each item to move, then click Confirm.

"To another location" sends items to another building's inbox, including a building on the same plot. This is a delivery. You pick the Destination and a delivery method. A delivery business charges a fee, paid from a wallet you choose. Otherwise your own Seedlings carry it.

You can also drag items to another container. With a container's context menu open, hold Shift to select items and drag them onto another container. Containers in the same building, or on the same plot outside any building, use a transfer. Outdoor Block tiles count as part of their building. Different buildings, a building and its plot, or different plots use a delivery. For a delivery, you can ask a delivery business to do it or have your own Seedlings carry it.

Seedlings carry transfers as hauling work. Give a Seedling the "Haul" priority in the Scheduler to have them do it. If they are not hauling, they probably lack permission to the source or destination container.

A transfer moves the amount you set and then ends, and it disappears from the Transfers list. It doesn't repeat. To keep a machine emptied as it produces, tick its products in a nearby container's Accepted Items instead, as described in Advanced Inventory Management.

To cancel a transfer, click the Transfers button (the route icon) in the header of the container it leaves from. The list shows that container's outgoing transfers. Click the trash icon next to one, or open it and click Remove.

#### Placement Mode
Placement Mode is how you position objects, like furniture or machine, onto your property. It allows you to move and rotate items from storage containers or existing locations. Noted that not all items in the world are placeable.

##### Accessing the Mode
- From an Inventory Container: Click the container (like the Kernel), select the placeable item, and click it to enter the mode.
- From Management Hub: Open the Properties Panel and select Storage(May not apply: !organization browser enabled.) , or open the Business Panel and go to its Inventory tab, then navigate to the building which holds the object you want to place. Select the item.
- From a Placed Object: Select an object that is already placed to open its context menu, hover the three-dots (⋮) button in the top right corner of the menu header, and choose "Move" in the panel that opens.

##### Using the Mode
Once in the mode, you can move the item and use Ctrl + Q/E to rotate it, or hold Ctrl to snap its alignment. You can only place objects within the bounds of your building or plot. Some items, such as wall paintings, are restricted to specific surfaces like walls.

##### Completing Placement
After selecting the spot, the object appears as a blue holograph. This signifies that a Seedling must now pick up, move, and assemble the item to complete the action. A nearby Seedling will automatically take the task, but you can also click the blue holograph to manually direct your Seedling to assemble it.

#### Furnish Mode
Furnish Mode is a dedicated mode for decorating and equipping a building's interior. While Placement Mode places or moves one object at a time, Furnish Mode lets you furnish whole rooms in one session: placing, moving, and rotating furniture and machines. Enter it with the "Furnish" button in the header of a building's context menu.

Inside Furnish Mode, available items are organized by category — tables, chairs, beds, lamps, machines, and so on — rather than by which container they are stored in, so you can browse by what you want to add. Rotate the selected object in small steps with Ctrl + Scroll or Ctrl + Q/E, or in quarter turns with Ctrl + Shift + Q/E. Switch floors with Shift + Scroll.

Placement is forgiving about tight spots, so chairs slide neatly up to tables. Keep in mind that an object's activity spots can be blocked by how you place it: a chair pushed with its front against a wall leaves no room to sit, so Seedlings won't use it. If a piece of furniture is being ignored, check that there is free space where a Seedling would stand or sit to use it.

To remove an object here, use its context menu the same way as outside Furnish Mode: Pack or Delete.

#### Inbox
The Inbox is a building's main input container where Seedlings will deposit harvested resources, crafted items, and delivered goods. When a Seedling completes a production task or receives an item from the Marketplace, it will go to the Inbox by default. It's essential for crafting and construction tasks, as machines and builders draw materials from here. Until a new storage container is crafted/bought, you will heavily rely on the Inbox for all your storage needs.(May not apply: organization browser enabled.)  Invitations and job applications go to a separate inbox, in the top bar of the Organizations window.

#### Outbox
The Outbox holds goods waiting to leave the building, for a sale or a delivery. You can't set what an Outbox accepts: a Seedling hauls goods there only for a sell order, a contract or a delivery. To have new goods go there automatically, set up Auto-Sell, a repeating sell order (see Building Market Page: Auto-Sell and Auto-Restock). Delivery Drones collect items from Outboxes to transport them to buyers, other buildings, or storage.

#### Mobile Transport Unit (MTU)
<!-- keywords: mtu vehicle walker truck cart storage drive move to park transport carry goods shove shoved push blocking in the way -->
*A walking vehicle with storage: driving it, who may use it, why it stops, and when Seedlings can shove it away.*

The MTU is a large, slow walker with its own storage. Seedlings haul goods into and out of it like any other storage container.

To drive it, select the MTU, choose "Move to..." in its context menu and click where it should go. Escape leaves the mode.

It stops in front of anything it cannot pass, such as trees, walls and placed objects. If it stops short, pick a spot with a clearer route.

The owner can move it and use its storage. In a society, business, or other organization, a member needs a role with the "Move vehicles" permission to move it. Seedlings with the "Seedlings use vehicles" permission take items from it and put items into it. Players open its storage and change its settings with the "Manage storage" permission, and move items between it and other containers with the "Plan deliveries" permission. A business's Owner(May not apply: organization browser enabled.)  and Partners have (Alternative; may not apply.)  has all of them. Its employees can use the storage but cannot move it. In a society, adults can use the storage, and moving it needs a leadership role.

It can be packed from the three-dots menu once its storage is empty, and placed again later. The MTU gets its ownership based on the location (building or plot) it's unpacked on.

An MTU left standing on society territory (never neutral territory), outside a building or plot its owner owns or rents, can be shoved away. Its context menu then shows a warning with a countdown. When the countdown ends, any adult Seedling can "Shove" the MTU to push it a few meters away. An MTU owned by the society whose territory it stands on is never shoveable.



### Deliveries
<!-- keywords: where is my order bought package arriving orbital storage progress -->
*Moving items between buildings, delivery drones, shipping speeds, and running a delivery service.*

Marketplace purchases are carried by the Delivery Business picked at purchase, or by your own Seedlings with Self Pickup. A delivery business moves goods with its employees and its delivery drones. More drones mean faster deliveries, and you can never have too many delivery drones (*quack* *quack*). To track what's in transit and the status of past deliveries, (May not apply: organization browser enabled.) select Deliveries under a building or plot in the Properties Panel's list (Alternative; may not apply.) use the Deliveries button at the top of the Properties Panel, or All deliveries on a building's page.
To move items to another building, open the building that holds them in the Properties Panel and select Storage(May not apply: !organization browser enabled.) , or in the Business Panel and go to its Inventory tab. Select the item, click Transfer and choose "To another location". The Transferring Items Between Containers section covers every option.
Delivery in Avesta takes time. If you have not seen an item in its designated container, it means the item is still in transit. A red arrow on an item's icon means it is being moved out of the container. A green arrow means it is being moved into the container. Note: that if you deliver to a destination with public access, items may be used by other Seedlings. Therefore, consider using a privately-owned building to ensure your items are secure and not lost or stolen.

If a delivery seems stuck or never arrives, check these in order: first, make sure the destination Inbox actually has free space, since a delivery cannot complete into a container that's full, so a permanently-stuck delivery is often a full Inbox rather than a lost one. Second, for a business handling external deliveries, someone has to be assigned to move it: at least one employee with the "Client Deliveries" work priority, or a Delivery Drone set to External, since without that assignment the delivery simply sits unclaimed rather than failing outright. Finally, if you still can't find an item, check whether it may have ended up in a different storage location your Seedling has access to (including any shared society storage your role permits) rather than the container you expected. A delivery coming from another society does not wait forever. If it has not arrived within [world-dependent value: import expiry real days] real-life days, it is failed and stopped. An old import that never showed up is gone, not still on its way.

Click a delivery in the Deliveries list to open its details. A delivery still inside your society can be stopped with Cancel Delivery. This is not possible once a Seedling or drone has been assigned to carry it. You also need permission to manage its containers. A delivery marked as Self Delivery must be picked up by your own Seedlings or by one of your drones in internal mode. A delivery also turns into Self Delivery when the delivery business assigned to it stops offering delivery. If it shows Send Seedling to pickup, select one of your Seedlings and click it. That Seedling then carries the items.

A Self Delivery into a building needs someone to haul it there. That is a Seedling scheduled for Hauling (the Haul priority) at that building, or a delivery drone set to Internal at that branch. A delivery drone only works for its own branch. Set to External, it carries the client deliveries assigned to its branch. Set to Internal, it hauls inside its branch and carries deliveries coming into it. It also carries its branch's sales to the market, exports to the Spaceport, and deliveries to your other buildings. If nobody picks up a delivery for a full tau, a warning triangle appears over the building, and a warning shows on the delivery in the building's Deliveries list. Set up a Seedling for Hauling there, or use Send Seedling to pickup. For a delivery business, the same warning on a client delivery means you should check that a Seedling there has the Client Deliveries priority.
#### Earning with a Delivery Service
Your business can earn SeedCoin by delivering items for other Cultivators. (May not apply: organization browser enabled.) In the Properties Panel, select Deliveries under your Business Branch and stay on its Overview tab. (Alternative; may not apply.) Open the Deliveries tab of your Business Branch and stay on its Overview tab. Switch Delivery Service to Enabled. Click Adjust to set your price per distance. The Overview tab also lists your delivery agents and how much of the tau their shifts cover. Its Performance area shows your delivery revenue and top clients. Assign employees or drones to external deliveries so they handle client deliveries. Agents that handle internal hauling move items in the building, handle deliveries coming in and going out to the spaceport and npc. When another Cultivator buys an item on the Marketplace, they can pick your delivery business to transport it. Your agents haul the item to the buyer and you earn based on the distance travelled.

How long a delivery takes depends on two things: the distance between your society and the seller's, and the Shipment Method chosen when the order is placed. Same-society purchases arrive almost immediately. For inter-society purchases (via the Spaceport and Global Market), three Shipment Methods trade off cost against speed. Economy is the slowest and cheapest. Standard is the middle ground. Express is the fastest and most expensive. Express is roughly 5× faster than Standard, and Standard is 10× faster than Economy. The trip between societies never takes longer than one tau, which is one real hour. The Shipment Method only changes this trip. After it, the goods land at your Spaceport and Seedlings still carry them to your building. Purchases from your own society don't go through the Spaceport.

### Building Market Page: Auto-Sell and Auto-Restock
*Selling and buying automatically for a building: Auto-Sell and Auto-Restock.*
<!-- keywords: auto buy automatic buy restock food recurring buy order auto sell -->

Every finished building you manage has a Market page in the Properties Panel. (May not apply: organization browser enabled.) Select Market under the building in the Properties Panel's list. (Alternative; may not apply.) Open it with the Market button at the top of the Properties Panel, or with Open market on the building's page. It is for buying and selling automatically. To list the building itself for sale or rent, use the Market block on the building's page. To buy or sell by hand, use the Marketplace (see Buying on the Marketplace and Selling on the Marketplace).
- Every building, including homes, has Auto-Restock (to buy).
- Buildings run by a business, a department or the society also have Auto-Sell (to sell).

#### Auto-Restock
To buy something automatically, open the building's Market page and go to Auto-Restock. Click "Set Up Auto-Restock". Choose the Resource, the Target stock, the Max price per unit, the wallet to Pay from and a Delivery Business. Whenever stock falls below the target, Auto-Restock buys more for you. It places orders a few times a tau, so the first purchase can take a while. There is no repeating Buy Order. If the wallet runs out of money, Auto-Restock shows "Idle" and starts again once there is money. If the building's Inbox has no capacity left for that resource, Auto-Restock shows "Inbox full" and starts again once the Inbox has capacity.

Auto-Restock only buys from your own society and from Seed Industries. To buy from another society, buy on the Marketplace yourself. To set up Auto-Restock you need the "Edit market orders" permission on the building. As the owner, you have it.

#### Auto-Sell
Auto-Sell keeps selling an item from the building's Outbox. Click "New Auto-Sell". Set the Price (per unit), the Max units in outbox, and in Sell on, whether to sell in your society only or to other societies too. Auto-Sell updates its listing a few times a tau.

### Contracts
<!-- keywords: contracts tab where moved missing gone business panel properties panel make contracts permission -->
*Trade agreements between businesses or government departments with terms, schedules, and fines.*

This management tool is only applicable in Business Branches. Contracts allow businesses to form structured trade agreements. These are not made between individuals, but between two business entities or government departments. (May not apply: organization browser enabled.) In the Properties Panel, under Contracts below the branch in the list (Alternative; may not apply.) In the Business Panel, under the Contracts tab, you can define what’s being traded (products or SeedCoin), under what conditions, and how often (once, daily, or twice daily). You can also define time limits, cancellation clauses, and fines. Contracts automate exchanges and encourage interdependence between specialized Cultivators and societies.(May not apply: organization browser enabled.)  The Contracts figure on the business's Overview in the Organizations window opens them too.

Exchanges run at noon and midnight in game time, not the moment a trade is due. Both sides then have 12 in-game hours to get ready. Haul the goods into the outbox within that window. The exchange completes on its own once both sides are ready, and fails if either side is not.

Either side can propose ending a contract early. The other party can accept or decline, and there is no time limit on their answer. You can also force the termination through straight away and pay the cancellation fine.
