# Having a Baby in SEED

Having children extends your legacy: children continue your line and inherit the properties when your Seedling dies. Here is what having a baby requires:

1. **A romantic partner**: the two Seedlings must be in a Dating or Partner relationship. Intimacy happens on a double bed: select the partner Seedling and choose the romantic action, or let the couple do it autonomously.
2. **Fertility**: use a **Pollen Pill** on your Seedling. To get one in-game: tap the **+ icon** in the bottom-left corner, select the Seedling you'd like to make fertile, and purchase the pill for [value needed] Pollen. Pollen Pills are also sometimes gifted in the official Discord channel.
3. **Residency**: your Seedling must be an assigned resident of a home. The first building your Seedling owns or rents is automatically set as their residence; if it was changed or removed, manage residents in the Properties Panel.
4. **A free Seedling Slot** for the new baby.

The child inherits traits from both parents, and the Cultivator who purchases the Fertility Pill becomes the owner of the new baby Seedling. The Pollen requirement for fertility is a mechanic that helps manage the world's population and server resources. Think of it as investing in your family's future generations.
