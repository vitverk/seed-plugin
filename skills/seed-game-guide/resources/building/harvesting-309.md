# Harvesting

Harvesting is the best way to gather resources for survival. Send your Seedling to harvest through **Direct Control** by clicking on natural resources in the world and selecting the harvest action. Some resources require a machine to extract. Some harvestables also have a chance to yield a **bonus drop** on top of their normal resource. Harvest actions show your success chance in the context menu, and a failed harvest now yields nothing, so skill and preparation matter.

**TIP:** To make harvesting more efficient, save resource harvests to a Seedling's **TODO List** (right-click the resource > "Add to TODO list"). Without TODO items, Seedlings harvest less consistently. This is especially important if you set harvesting as a Scheduler priority: save the specific resources so your Seedling knows what to focus on. TODO harvesting is for natural resources (trees, stones, shrubs). Farm crops are managed from the Farming section instead.

**Who Can Harvest Where**

Any Seedling in a society can harvest natural resources from **anywhere within that society's territory**, regardless of whose plot the resource sits on. Harvesting is only blocked across societies. A Seedling cannot harvest in another society's territory.

When a resource is harvested, the pile drops on the ground at that plot and **inherits the plot owner's ownership**: on someone else's plot, it becomes their property. The harvester gets a short exclusive window to pick the pile up first; if they don't collect it in time, the pile is no longer accessible to them. This is why a Seedling can sometimes harvest something and then find it "belongs to someone else".

Note for employers: you can set harvesting as an employee job task, but only for resources located on your own business's property.
