# What is SEED?

SEED is a society simulation where you, the Cultivator, guide the lives of your Seedlings in a massive, persistent world called Avesta. Your actions have a significant impact on your society: the choices your Seedlings make, from their careers to their relationships, shape the world for all other players.

Your ultimate goal is to guide your Seedlings as they are born, grow, and eventually die, helping them achieve their dreams. Their legacy is your creation.

Two things make SEED different from most games. First, time moves fast: one in-game day equals one real-world hour. Second, the world is persistent: your Seedlings continue to live, work, and follow their schedule even when you are logged off, so make sure their needs are taken care of and their schedule is set up before you leave.

As the Cultivator, you are a planner and designer of life on Avesta, managing your Seedlings' needs, schedules, employment, society roles, and more. You are not seen by the Seedlings, but you can chat with them or give them commands through Seedling Chat (or by Direct Control). You play alongside other Cultivators, and communicating and engaging with others is key to helping your Seedlings survive the harsh conditions of Avesta. Everything you create on the planet must be carried out by a Seedling in the end. From building things to moving them around, all happenings on Avesta are completed by Seedlings.
