# What Are Seedlings?

Seedlings the characters in the game,they have their own agency and autonomy. They are living, breathing organisms that grow, age, work, socialize, and eventually die, making their own choices within the world you help shape.

Each Seedling has needs, a mood, skills, stats, jobs, relationships, interests, and perks, each of which influences their independent decisions.

Seedlings can live up to 120 years (1 year in seed is 1 real life day, so 120 days)  but may die earlier from starvation, thirst, exhaustion, or diseases or injuries. Seedlings cannot be deleted, they can die from neglect, but that is a cruel thing to do. And we don’t want to be cruel now do we!?
