# Mood

A Seedling's mood is influenced by need fulfillment and their current environment. Check their **Thoughts** on the Seedling Card to understand it. Depressed Seedlings slouch and look sad; happy Seedlings stand tall and walk proudly.

For a deeper look, open the Mind tab in Focus Mode (or just click your Seedling's mood face or thought bubble). It shows their mood as a living landscape where each thought pulls it up or down. A Seedling's mood is driven by their strongest thoughts, so one big worry or joy matters more than a pile of small ones.
