# Production Chains

To optimize production, think through every step a Seedling must take from raw material to finished product. You design the system and set up tasks; Seedlings carry them out. Things don't move around by themselves. Every step needs a Seedling.

**The Three Stages of Production**

1. **Resource Collection**: Seedlings gather raw materials (wood, stone) from the world, or you buy materials from the Marketplace.
2. **Hauling & Processing**: Seedlings move materials from storage and operate machines to process them into finished goods.
3. **Output & Delivery**: Seedlings move finished goods from the machine's output to another storage container, ready for use or sale.

**Assigning Production Tasks to Employees**

Recruit Seedlings and assign job task priorities with the Employment tool in the Business Panel: select the activity type to prioritize (e.g., "Produce"), then specify the item (e.g., "Produce Algae Paste"). The assigned Seedling will only do the top priority; if it's impossible (e.g., missing materials) they move to their second task. Design your priority system with the whole chain in mind.

**Preventing Work Interruption**

- Seedlings pause work for urgent needs (hunger, thirst), so provide food and water facilities **near the workplace** so breaks stay short.
- If no assigned priority is possible, employees just hang around. Ensure machines are **turned on**, all required **materials** are available, **production tasks are set on machines**, and there are **enough machines** that Seedlings aren't queuing for equipment.
- Observe your employees at work. It's the best way to learn how to manage their jobs effectively.

For large-scale production across multiple machines, use the **Crafting management tool** (in your Business Branch, and in the Properties Panel): create tasks per machine and keep an overview of all of them. It doesn't yet cover resource-gathering in the wild (harvesting or mining). Remember the harvesting restriction: employees can only be tasked to harvest resources on your own business property.
