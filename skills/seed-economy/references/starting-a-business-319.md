# Starting a Business

Creating a business requires holding a role that grants the right to own a business, typically the default **Adult** role, or a custom role the Mayor has granted that right to. Only Seedlings can directly own a business (societies, departments, and groups cannot).

1. Open the **Business Panel** (right sidebar) and click **"Create Account"**.
2. Choose which of your Seedlings owns the business, pick a name, and pay the creation fee from that Seedling's wallet. The business wallet is separate from your Seedling's personal wallet and is shared across all your business branches.
3. Acquire a **Business Branch**: buy a plot or building from the Real Estate Market **using your business wallet** (ownership follows the paying wallet), construct a new building on an empty plot via the Land Planner, rent a building with the business wallet, or reassign a plot/building your Seedling already owns to the business.
4. The branch gives you an inbox and outbox for deliveries and unlocks the business management tabs: Overview, Employment, Production, Services, Market, Inventory, Contracts, and Deliveries.

Businesses are organizations with their own roles and permissions: as owner you can create roles and assign them to employees to control what they may do, such as managing production, accessing storage, or spending business funds.

**Ways a Business Makes Money**

- Hire employees and run production, then sell the goods.
- Run a **service location** (cafe, gym, spa) offering advertised services that visiting Seedlings pay for, plus an optional entry fee. Set it up in the Services tab: start the location, choose services and prices, set opening hours and entry fee, and manage guest room access.
- Deliver items for other Cultivators and get paid per distance.
- Trade on the Marketplace with Sell Orders, Buy Orders, and Contracts.
- Own plots and buildings, rent them out, or resell them.
