# Inbox, Outbox & Deliveries

### Inbox & Outbox

- **Inbox**: a building's main input container. Seedlings deposit harvested resources, crafted items, and delivered goods here; completed production tasks and Marketplace purchases arrive here by default. Machines and builders draw materials from it. Until you craft or buy more storage, you'll rely heavily on the Inbox.
- **Outbox**: stores items ready for pickup, delivery, or sale. When a crafting task completes or a Sell Order is created, a Seedling places the item in the Outbox; Delivery Drones collect from Outboxes to transport to buyers, other buildings, or storage.

**Deliveries**

Initially the Society Kernel acts as temporary Delivery HQ with Seedlings as delivery personnel. Once a **Delivery HQ Antenna** (Marketplace) is affixed to a building, that building becomes a Delivery HQ with **Delivery Drones**: its benefits are available to all society members. Drones can be crafted or bought; fewer drones means slower deliveries, and you can never have too many. Track what's in transit in a building's **Deliveries tab**.

To move items between buildings: open the Properties or Business Panel of the building holding the item, go to the Inventory tab, click the item, and select **"Move to Another Container"**: it's delivered automatically by your delivery personnel or drones. You can also simply drag resources between containers on the same plot, or drag items from your Seedling's inventory onto a nearby container and they'll walk over and drop them off. Just make sure a Seedling has the Haul or Deliver priority set (or use direct control) so someone actually carries it. Delivery takes time: a **red dot** on an item's icon means it's being moved out of the box, a **green dot** means it's being moved in. If you deliver to a destination with public access, other Seedlings may use the items, so consider a privately owned building to keep things secure.

**Delivery speed** depends on distance and the shipping option chosen at purchase. Same-society purchases arrive almost immediately. Inter-society purchases (via Spaceport and Global Market) offer three options: **Budget** (slowest, cheapest), **Basic** (default), and **Express** (fastest, most expensive, roughly five times faster than Basic, which is in turn ten times faster than Budget).

**Earning with a Delivery Service**

Your business can earn SeedCoin delivering for other Cultivators: open your branch's Deliveries tab > Delivery Agents, set the service to **"Open"**, choose your price per distance, and assign employees or drones to **external** deliveries (internal agents only move items within your own location). When another Cultivator buys on the Marketplace, they can pick your delivery business; you earn by distance traveled.
