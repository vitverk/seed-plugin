# Contracts

Contracts allow businesses to form structured trade agreements. They are not made between individuals but between **two business entities or government departments**. This management tool is only applicable in Business Branches.

In the Business Panel, under the **Contracts** tab, you define:

- What's being traded: products or SeedCoin.
- Under what conditions.
- How often: once, daily, or twice daily.
- Time limits, cancellation clauses, and fines.

Contracts automate exchanges and encourage interdependence between specialized Cultivators and societies.
