# Inviting Players to Your Society

There is no direct invite button in the game, but there are a few good ways to bring players in:

- **Found together:** create a private lobby with a password and share it with the players you want.
- **Migration:** players join an existing society by migrating a Seedling there; they need a Migration Ticket, or by creating a new Seedling in your society. If your society is public, prospective members can click it in World View and select "Visit" to look around first.
- **Make arrival easy:** set empty plots as **claimable** so newcomers get free starter land, and consider a **Campus Residence Billboard** to give Seedlings without a home a public residence, which helps new arrivals feel less stressed as they settle in. The Society Info overview also shows how many claimable plots your society has free for new joiners.
- **Recruit in Players Chat:** the chat reaches Cultivators across the globe and is the place to find and welcome new members.
