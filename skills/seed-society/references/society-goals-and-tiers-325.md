# Society Goals & Tiers

Your society's development is guided by a structured progression system. You progress through **tiers** by completing **Society Goals**: collective objectives all members work towards together. Goals start with essential survival needs before advancing to more complex systems like governance and trade. The six tiers, in order: **Expedition, Camp, Settlement, Town, City, Metropolis**.

- Open the **Society Panel** (right sidebar) > Society Goals tab to see the active goals and announcements from your Society Leader.
- Goals are **tracked automatically**: once a goal's requirements are met it is marked complete. When all goals for the current tier are finished, the society automatically advances to the next tier, unlocking new capabilities, governance roles, permissions, and increased population capacity. More advanced features, such as government types and voting, unlock as your society advances.
- To contribute: check what the goals need and pitch in. In the early phase that usually means gathering resources and meeting basic needs. Participating in society development can also open opportunities to earn SeedCoin.
- Every society and environment is different, and Players Chat is a great resource for learning how others are completing goals and objectives.
- You can **pin a society goal** to the HUD to follow its completion in real time.

**TIP:** Read the **Lexicon**: click the book icon at the top of the Society window for the article about Societies. Lexicon articles explain the deeper systems of the world.
