# Migration

Seedlings can physically walk to a nearby society, but they cannot interact with that society's Seedlings or objects. To participate in another society, a Seedling must **migrate** there.

1. (Optional) Scout first: open **World View**, click a public society, and select **"Visit"** to enter Visitor Mode and look around before deciding.
2. Click your society's **Kernel**, choose **Inspect**, then click **Migrate** in the inspector panel.
3. Choose the destination society. Migration requires a **Migration Ticket**: your Seedling receives some when they are created.
4. Your Seedling arrives at the destination society's Kernel.

**Restrictions:** Seedlings cannot migrate if they are a child, pregnant, or have dependent children (infants or kids). You will be warned if your Seedling owns real estate, owns a business, holds a society role, leads a group, or has active relationships, so prepare your Seedling for the change beforehand. Migration also respects the destination sector's population limit, so a full sector can't be joined.

- The "I'm stuck" onboarding button offers **"Move to another society"** as a shortcut to the migration interface. It's the right choice if another player is deliberately blocking your progress.
- Once a **Spaceport** is built, **Orbital Storage** can be used during migration to save and ship resources to other societies across the planet.
- Alternatively, start over by creating a new Seedling in another society instead of migrating.
