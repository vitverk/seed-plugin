# Is there controller support? Can I play across devices?

SEED plays on PC and Mac, with a mobile Companion App for iOS and Android to check in on your Seedlings wherever you are. Because SEED is one shared world that lives on our servers, there's nothing to sync, sign in on your computer or the app and you're in the same Avesta, right where you left off.

Controller support isn't available at the moment; SEED is played with mouse and keyboard for now.
