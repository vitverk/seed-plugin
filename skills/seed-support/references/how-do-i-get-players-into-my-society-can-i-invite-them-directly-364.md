# How do I get players into my society? Can I invite them directly?

When you start a society, you'll create a lobby. Set it to private with a password and share that password with the friends you want to build with (you need at least 3 Cultivators to found a society). A new society can host 3 Seedlings to begin with, and welcomes more as it grows.

You can also keep your society public, others can find it in World View and move in, either by migrating an existing Seedling over (using a Migration Ticket) or by creating a new Seedling in your society. New arrivals land at your society's Kernel. You can even share a direct link to your society so players can join you straight away.

There's no one-click "invite" button, but spreading the word is easy: recruit players in General Chat (it reaches Cultivators all over Avesta) or share your society on our Discord.
