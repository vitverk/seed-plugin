# How do I report a player?

If someone's breaking the rules, report them in-game using **Report Players**, this covers harassment, discrimination, cheating, and other misconduct. You can also reach us through the support portal or Discord with the player's Seedling name and what happened; screenshots help. All reports are confidential and reviewed by our moderation team. To report someone, press ESC and follow the reporting procedure.

The support team will receive your report, investigate it fully and update you of the details once the investigation has taken place.
