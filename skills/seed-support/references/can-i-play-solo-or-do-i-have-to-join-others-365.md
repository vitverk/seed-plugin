# Can I play solo, or do I have to join others?

You don't need to bring anyone with you to get started. Most new Cultivators simply join one of our recommended societies, which have plenty of space and resources to welcome you, and you can play at your own pace alongside the community that's already there.

If you'd rather build your own from the ground up, founding a brand-new society takes at least 3 Cultivators together.

SEED is at its best when it's shared, but there's plenty of room to play it your way.
