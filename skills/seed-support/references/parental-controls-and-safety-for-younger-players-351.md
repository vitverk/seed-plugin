# Parental controls and safety for younger players

SEED is a world of real people interacting in real time, and it carries a provisional PEGI 16 rating. There are currently no dedicated parental control settings, and age is self-declared at sign-up. If you're a parent or guardian, we'd encourage you to consider that live, real-player element, including open in-game chat with other players, when deciding whether SEED is right for a younger player. Every player can report misconduct through Report Players, and all reports are reviewed by our moderation team. For our full community standards, see the SEED Rules and Community Guidelines.
