# Is there a minimum age to play?

SEED currently has a provisional PEGI 16 rating (the official licence is still pending confirmation from PEGI), so it's intended for players aged 16 and over. This provisional rating reflects the following PEGI content descriptors: Violence, Bad Language, Sex, Drugs, and In-Game Purchases.

SEED is also a world of real people interacting in real time, building relationships, forming societies, and chatting with other players through in-game chat. If you're a parent or guardian, it's worth keeping that live, real-player element in mind when deciding whether SEED is right for a younger player.

We'll update this once PEGI confirms the final rating.
