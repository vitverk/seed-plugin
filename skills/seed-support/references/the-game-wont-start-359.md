# The game won't start

1. Restart your PC.
2. Verify your game files (Steam: right-click SEED → Properties → Installed Files → Verify integrity of game files).
3. Update your graphics driver.
4. Check your PC against the system requirements.
5. Reset your local SEED settings, then restart (see the crashing article for the exact Windows/Mac steps).
6. Still nothing? Uninstall and reinstall the game.
