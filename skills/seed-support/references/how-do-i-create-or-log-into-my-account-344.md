# How do I create or log into my account?

To play SEED you'll need a SEED account from [seed.game](https://seed.game) From the sign-in screen, you can get in a few ways:

- **Email and password** - enter your email and password, and tick "Keep me signed in" if you'd like to stay logged in on your device.
- **Magic link** - choose "Login with a magic link" and we'll email you a secure link that signs you in.
- **Continue with Apple, Google, Discord, or Steam** - sign in with an account you already have; pick the provider and approve the connection.

New to SEED? Signing in for the first time with any of these options creates your account.
