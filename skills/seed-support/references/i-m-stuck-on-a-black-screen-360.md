# I'm stuck on a black screen

1. Give it a minute, the game can take longer to load right after an update.
2. Alt+Tab out and back in.
3. Switch between fullscreen, windowed, and borderless in your display settings.
4. Disconnect any second monitor and try again.
5. Update your graphics driver.
6. Still black? Reset your local SEED settings.
