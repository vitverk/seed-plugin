# Plots and building

Observed in SEED staging, October 2026. Verify current access and boundaries.

- Group membership and plot ownership are distinct: transferring a plot can
  change ownership and management of its equipment, so use the user's actual
  authorization and inspect the resulting owner. Do not control another
  player's Seedling merely because it belongs to the same group.
- Read plot cell bounds and placement responses; map centers alone do not prove
  continuity or a valid location. Check what is owned before moving objects.
  Empty a container before moving it, provide a usable sleeping alternative and
  verify both the new object and the cleared site after the move.
- Clearing vegetation can have multiple stages. In one trial, harvesting a
  landscape tree converted it to an entity with further harvests, then a stump
  that required removal. An empty landscape scan alone did not prove clearance.
  Inspect spawned entities, remaining harvests and loose piles. For landscape
  actions use the exact tile and grid cell returned by the current tools.
- Inspect a blueprint's material requirements, blocking objects and inbox;
  supply and construction are separate steps. Keep materials already committed
  to other projects visible. Cancelling a new construction can lose delivered
  materials according to the game guide; do not cancel casually.
- The building guide describes editing existing buildings in Build Mode and
  adding windows. Daylight does not replace night lighting. Some equipment may
  wear faster outdoors; inspect its current durability display. Separate
  fire-prone machinery from flammable furnishings when planning interiors.
