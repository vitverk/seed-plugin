# Care and recovery

Evidence: real SEED staging, October 2026. Revalidate in the current build and
world. The [health guide](https://klang-games.eu.theymes.com/hc/en/seed/articles/health-conditions-307)
is a source snapshot, not proof of every current mechanic.

- Check each controlled Seedling's health, needs, current activity and queue at
  an interval suited to the user's wishes and the situation. For injuries,
  inspect severity, body part, treatment, doses used and next treatment time.
  Choose duties and recovery proportionately, and recheck before restoring work.
- The health guide says injuries heal naturally and do not worsen; illnesses may
  worsen. Failed work can cause a separate injury: hot or chemical processes
  can burn, sharp work can cut, heavy work can strain, and mining can irritate.
  Lower skill and poor light can increase accident risk. Do not assert a minor
  injury always requires full rest. Honor explicit rest instructions and check
  actual autonomous activity; a schedule is not an absolute prohibition.
- Treatment can require multiple doses, a cooldown, supplies, another Seedling
  or a skill. Read the live condition and action eligibility. Verify doses and
  changed health or recovery time rather than assuming a queued treatment worked.
- Treat water as a chain: accessible source, raw collection, filter inputs,
  filtration, clean stock and consumption. Food similarly requires ingredients,
  an available cooking station, preparation, accessible output and consumption.
  Inspect current stock, reservations and people at each link. A queued drink or
  meal is not proof that its intended recipient consumed it.
- Put food, water, bladder and rest ahead of work; preserve sleep and allow care
  time. Stock targets must cover consumers and replenishment gaps; size buffers
  from observation, not a universal bottle-per-person assumption. A full-day
  self-sustaining care economy has not yet been established in these trials.
- If a tent is moved or removed, update the affected sleep location and verify
  actual sleep and rising rest at the replacement location.
